Digestive health and gut literacy

Understand what gas, bloating, constipation, and diarrhea can mean; evaluate fibre and probiotic claims; and use food experiments without turning them into permanent restriction.

Start with safety: Severe pain, significant bleeding, collapse, confusion, repeated vomiting with inability to hydrate, or signs of obstruction or shock needs urgent care. A food diary should never delay assessment.
