Using the meal finder

How are my meals picked?

Your answers help us sort the meals. Compare the nutrition and see which ones fit your goals.

Can I pick more than one goal?

Yes. Try weight gain and high protein, for example. Each result shows how many of your goals it matches.

Do I need a calorie target?

No. You can use the finder right away. The macro calculator can help if you want a daily estimate.

Will my order have these exact numbers?

Portions and swaps can change the numbers. Check the restaurant’s menu and allergen information before ordering.
