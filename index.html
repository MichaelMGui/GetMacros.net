The three macronutrients

"Macros" is short for macronutrients — the three nutrients your body needs in large amounts for energy and to build and repair tissue. Each one plays a different, non-interchangeable role.

Protein
💪

Building & repairing tissue

Made of amino acids, protein builds and repairs muscle, skin, and organs, and forms enzymes, antibodies, and hormones. 4 calories per gram.

 Fat
🧬

Hormones & cell structure

Dietary fat builds cell membranes, produces steroid hormones like testosterone and estrogen, and carries vitamins A, D, E, and K. 9 calories per gram.

 Carbohydrates
⚡

Energy & glycogen

Carbs are your body's preferred fuel, stored as glycogen in muscle and liver and used directly by your brain. 4 calories per gram.

Why "just eat healthy" isn't specific enough

Calories tell you how much energy you're eating. Macros tell you what that energy is made of — and that composition affects muscle growth, hormone production, energy levels, and how full you feel. Two people eating the same 2,000 calories can have completely different outcomes depending on their protein, fat, and carb split.
