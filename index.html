Before you order

A few things worth knowing.

Find a useful match—not a one-size-fits-all “healthiest” meal.

Find my restaurant meal →
How does the meal finder choose my matches?

Your goals, appetite, dietary preferences and restaurant choices shape the ranking. Results show the nutrition numbers and explain where an order fits—and where it falls short.

Can I choose more than one goal?

Yes. Combine goals such as high protein and bulking in the quiz. Check each result’s explanation: a close match may meet some of your preferences rather than all of them.

Do I need to know my calorie target first?

No. You can start with your meal preferences. If you want a daily starting estimate, use the macro calculator separately; a daily target is not a target for every meal.

Will my exact order have the same nutrition?

Not necessarily. Published figures describe standard builds; portions, substitutions and location can change them. Check the restaurant’s current nutrition and allergen information before ordering. See our data sources.
