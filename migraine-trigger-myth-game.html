How it works

Eight decisions test common migraine nutrition claims. Your score stays here. Answer each prompt, read why the answer is what it is, and move on. There is no timer, no sign-up and nothing is saved — you can restart whenever you like.

What this covers

Each round gives you a claim to judge, then explains the reasoning behind the answer rather than just marking it right or wrong. Statements you will weigh up include:

  • Everyone with migraine must avoid chocolate and cheese.
  • Sudden worst-ever headache needs urgent assessment.
  • Caffeine can both help and contribute to headache.
  • More water always stops a migraine.
  • Cravings may begin before headache pain.
  • Migraine magnesium doses should be clinician-guided.
  • Eliminating many foods at once proves the trigger.
  • Butterbur is a risk-free natural choice.

Why bother

Recognising a shaky nutrition claim is a more durable skill than memorising a food list, because the claims change every year and the reasoning does not. If you would rather read first, browse the guide library or try a quiz.
