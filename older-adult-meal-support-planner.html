A conversation organizer, not a screening score

Older adult meal support planner

Select the barriers affecting meals. The planner keeps only the relevant support ideas and questions, ready to print or bring to a clinician, dietitian, pharmacist, dentist, social worker, occupational therapist, or speech-language pathologist.

Get urgent help for active choking or breathing difficulty. Sudden confusion, fainting, inability to keep fluids down, severe weakness, or other emergency symptoms also need prompt medical care.
What is affecting meals?

All eight support areas are shown.

No answers are saved or sent anywhere.

Low appetite or early fullness

Practical support

Offer smaller meals and snacks on a predictable schedule. Add protein and energy to familiar foods rather than making portions look larger.

Question for the right professional

Ask about nausea, constipation, pain, mood, medication changes, taste, and recent weight or strength changes.

Shopping, money, or food access

Practical support

List foods that are affordable, shelf-stable, easy to open, and actually enjoyed. Explore grocery delivery, community meals, benefits, family rotations, or transportation.

Question for the right professional

Ask a social worker, community agency, or care team about local meal and food-access programs.

Fatigue, mobility, or cooking difficulty

Practical support

Use frozen vegetables, canned beans or fish, eggs, prepared grains, rotisserie chicken when appropriate, and batch-cooked portions. Place frequently used items within safe reach.

Question for the right professional

Ask occupational therapy about kitchen setup, grip, standing tolerance, and safer appliances if daily tasks are changing.

Read the related guide →

Teeth, dentures, dry mouth, or chewing pain

Practical support

Choose moist, tender foods while arranging dental or medical assessment. Sauces, soups, yogurt, eggs, beans, fish, tofu, and soft cooked produce can reduce effort.

Question for the right professional

Ask a dentist or clinician about pain, denture fit, dry mouth, mouth sores, and medicine effects.

Read the related guide →

Coughing, choking, or swallowing difficulty

Practical support

Do not invent a thickened-liquid or texture plan. Follow assessed instructions, support upright positioning as directed, and allow enough time without distraction.

Question for the right professional

Request swallowing assessment from a speech-language pathologist; involve a dietitian to preserve hydration and nutrition.

Read the related guide →

Drinking too little or difficulty reaching fluids

Practical support

Pair drinks with meals and routine events, keep an easy-to-hold cup within reach, and address bathroom access. Count suitable soups and other fluids when allowed.

Question for the right professional

Confirm whether heart, kidney, liver, or sodium conditions require a fluid limit or special plan.

Read the related guide →

Eating alone, grief, or low motivation

Practical support

Schedule a shared meal, phone call, community lunch, or familiar ritual. Make one eating occasion social rather than trying to transform the entire day.

Question for the right professional

Ask about depression, grief support, cognitive changes, transportation, and local congregate-meal programs.

Read the related guide →

Unplanned weight or strength loss

Practical support

Record recent weights, meal changes, symptoms, and function. Use compact protein- and energy-containing foods while arranging timely assessment.

Question for the right professional

Bring the timeline and medication list to a clinician. Rapid loss, weakness, dehydration, bleeding, or confusion needs prompt care.

Read the related guide →

How to use this plan

Choose the barriers that are actually present, print the focused list, and add concrete examples: when the change began, recent weights if known, meals missed, choking or digestive symptoms, medication changes, and loss of strength or function.

This tool does not diagnose malnutrition, dehydration, dysphagia, depression, dementia, or another condition. It does not calculate fluid, calorie, protein, or supplement prescriptions.
