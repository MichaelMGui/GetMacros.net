How the amounts are calculated

A listed portion uses a measure with a weight in the source. For 100 calories, the tool divides by the source energy per 100 g and multiplies by 100. A missing amount or unavailable conversion stays unknown. The results show total nutrient content, which can differ from the amount absorbed.

These are selected U.S. reference foods. Use the exact preparation and source when comparing your own meal. For choline examples, see the separate egg, soybeans and quinoa comparison.

The USDA source column gives the record ID and links to the SR Legacy download.
