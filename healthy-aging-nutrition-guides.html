Healthy-aging nutrition guides

Appetite, food access, hydration, muscle, absorption, food safety, weight change, teeth, and swallowing can all reshape a meal. These guides solve practical problems without treating aging as a diagnosis.

Start with the cause, not a restriction. Sudden weight loss, choking, confusion, dehydration, weakness, or persistent appetite change deserves professional assessment. General healthy-eating advice cannot identify the reason.
