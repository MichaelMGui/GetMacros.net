Honey Nut Cheerios

Product Description

Honey Nut Cheerios is an un-bee-lievably delicious gluten-free breakfast cereal made of whole grain oats and made with real honey. Each 1 cup serving delivers 22g of whole grain (at least 48g recommended daily), 3g of fiber, and 12 essential vitamins and minerals, including 20% of your Daily Value of Vitamin D, 20% Daily Value of Iron, and 20% Daily Value of several B vitamins. It contains 12g of sugar per serving, which can be balanced within a meal by pairing this heart-healthy, gluten-free cereal with unsweetened milk, plain Greek yogurt, or fresh fruit, or mix in Original Cheerios for a lower-sugar bowl.

Honey Nut Cheerios is made with no artificial flavors and artificial colors. Ingredients like real honey, natural almond flavor, and whole grain oats gives every spoonful a clean, honey-forward taste. Honey Nut Cheerios can help lower cholesterol* as part of a heart healthy diet. *Three grams of soluble fiber daily from whole grain oat foods, like Honey Nut Cheerios Cereal, in a diet low in saturated fat and cholesterol, may reduce the risk of heart disease. Honey Nut Cheerios Cereal provides .75g per serving.

A delicious and nutritious option for kids and adults, Honey Nut Cheerios works as a versatile pantry staple and snack useful for everything from topping yogurt parfaits to making homemade granola bars and trail mix.

Allergens / Disclaimers

Contains almond ingredients.

Benefits / Consumer Statements

  • MADE WITH REAL HONEY: Sweetened with real honey, enhanced with natural almond flavor, and made with whole grain oats as the first ingredient. No artificial flavors or artificial colors. These ingredients give every spoonful a clean, honey-forward taste that delights both kids and adults.
  • CAN HELP LOWER CHOLESTEROL* as part of a heart healthy diet; *3g of soluble fiber daily from whole grain oat foods, in a diet low in saturated fat and cholesterol, may reduce the risk of heart disease; Honey Nut Cheerios provides .75g per serving
  • GLUTEN-FREE BREAKFAST & BEYOND: Serve with cold milk for breakfast, top with sliced banana or fresh berries for added fiber, or pair with Greek yogurt for extra protein. Add Honey Nut Cheerios to homemade granola bars, trail mix, and lunchbox treats.
  • FORTIFIED WHOLE GRAIN CEREAL: Start your day with 22g of whole grain (48g recommended daily) and 12 essential vitamins and minerals. Every serving includes 20% Daily Value of Vitamin D and 20% Daily Value of Iron, plus a healthy serving of calcium and fiber.
  • CONTAINS: One 10.8 oz box of Honey Nut Cheerios includes approximately 11 servings per box at the standard 1 cup serving size. Start your day off right or enhance your snacks with heart-healthy, gluten-free Honey Nut Cheerios.
