## Audit and backlog choices

The source-only inventory covered 350 public HTML routes, with 183 article-family candidates. It found 119 exact repeated paragraph groups and no near-identical first substantive introductions at the documented 0.72 SequenceMatcher threshold. This does not establish that all paragraphs are editorially good or all pages were visually inspected.

The highest-frequency groups are necessary disclosures (fictional game/example values, source editions, JavaScript alternatives and local storage), not slogans to delete blindly. Game controls and related shared copy are owned by root’s visual/interaction work. These findings are in `editorial-duplicate-copy.json`; the full extracted route text is in `editorial-source-inventory.json`.

For the 21 owned article routes, selected original introductions and relevant sections were read before choosing the improvement. The existing measurement, recipe and calculator explanations are retained. An obsolete blanket “all figures are invented” disclosure is corrected where a real sourced example is added. The old saved-order instruction is clarified to preserve historical diary snapshots. The redundant diary-guide subtitle is removed.

`editorial-backlog.json` records the eleven new purposes, ten improved established URLs, merged candidates and blocked candidates. Outlines, blocked questions and research topics are not counted as new published articles.

## Limits and maintenance

No restaurant or grocery nutrition record is newly imported by this editorial generator. It uses dated evidence already published by the product and factual source checks listed here. New-source retrieval is not automatically independent verification of an entire menu. Source edition, retrieval, package identity and local availability remain separate.

Canadian Subway’s current PDF request timed out. A search-result mention of an October edition was not verified, so no October values or update date were invented. Tim Hortons and New York Fries PDF requests succeeded; selected Tim Hortons rows were rechecked in extracted current pages. New York Fries current bytes match the previously visually reviewed source.

No branded-image or restaurant photography rights are assumed. These articles use purpose-built text and comparison tables. No medical claim, personalized target or extreme-diet advice is added. No clinician, dietitian, kitchen test, current-package inspection or first-hand taste comparison is claimed.

Maintenance triggers: a source edition or exact product label changes; recipe families/serving assumptions change; restaurant registry IDs change; country coverage gains a newly supported component; unit definitions change. The article arithmetic gates deliberately fail when checked fixture values diverge so a replacement requires review.
