## New York Fries Canada: reviewed selection

The current [Canadian fries page](https://www.newyorkfries.com/fries) and its footer
both link the [two-page nutrition guide](https://www.newyorkfries.com/_files/ugd/bdf8b6_4b1456a15baf4c13acd16438966d77e6.pdf).
The source was retrieved October 13 and both pages were actually rendered and
read. Both pages print “Last updated: Aug. 20, 2026.” Retrieval is not a new
nutrition edition.

Pinned PDF SHA-256:
26200ed27de3c04fb7165e7f6a1dd67251bf954640f2695f090d2b42a1776ee4

The new fact fixture contains 25 individually reviewed Canadian serving rows:
17 named foods/sides and eight extra ingredient amounts. Two additional orders
sum one source hot dog with one source fries portion. Thus the integration adds
27 records, of which 19 support the existing finder. Components remain separate.
These are selected observations, not a complete copied menu or local inventory.

The guide supplies actual gram weights, calories, all four core macros, fibre,
total sugar, saturated fat, sodium and some additional minerals. Original names,
sizes and source weights remain attached. Neither a Canadian serving nor a U.S.
serving is substituted for the other.

Extra portions apply to a snack-size poutine. The guide specifies twice the
extra amount for regular poutine and three times for large. That rule applies
to the added ingredient, not the whole order. It does not authorize other
customizations. Dips, drinks and unrecorded condiments are excluded from the two
GetMacros-calculated hot-dog orders.

Only selected factual names and numeric quantities are distributed. The source
PDF, its arrangement, prose, photographs, logo and artwork are not republished.
No open data license, partnership or ownership of restaurant marks is claimed.
The reviewed Canadian source page has a privacy link and public nutrition
download; it does not supply a reusable whole-catalogue license.

Source render files: restaurant-sources/nyf-current-1.png and
restaurant-sources/nyf-current-2.png. Raw PDF/source pages are research evidence
and must remain excluded from production hosting.

## Search intent observations

Queries inspected on October 13: “New York Fries Canada nutrition poutine
calories protein,” “New York Fries fries snack regular nutrition,” and “compare
restaurant nutrition Canada USA New York Fries.” Locale was not fixed by the
search tool; these are observed page types, not measured Canadian demand.

Results include the official Canadian poutine page, the separate U.S. nutrition
site, older community database servings and third-party complete-menu tables.
Some surfaced tables use different serving masses and older products. GetMacros
can provide source-edition and portion clarity, exact grams, two explicitly
calculated orders and honest missing-field handling instead of copying those
tables or claiming “every item.”

Useful primary follow-up action: choose the exact Canadian size in the finder,
compare its documented portion, then save or log it. The new restaurant guide
should answer size/portion questions near the top and expose the source. No
volume, difficulty, ranking, traffic, backlink or revenue metrics were inferred.
No authorized Search Console export was available to this subtask.
