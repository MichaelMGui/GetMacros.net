# Current search and source review — October 13, 2026

This is qualitative research, not a keyword-volume or traffic measurement. No authorized analytics or Search Console connector/export is available in this task. The earlier visitor increase cannot be attributed to organic search, and current clicks, CTR, indexing and country/device acquisition remain unmeasured.

## Primary guidance actually inspected

| Current source | Relevant observation | Applied decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) | Practical utility, accuracy and transparent production matter more than producing many superficially different articles. | One substantive grocery navigator, actual label search and exact local servings; no page for every barcode or keyword permutation. Preserve source dates rather than refreshing dates for appearance. |
| [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) | Doorways, keyword repetition and scaled content intended to manipulate rankings are inappropriate. | No city pages, fake reviews, synthetic traffic or interchangeable country articles. |
| [Localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions) | Equivalent regional pages need self-references, reciprocal links and absolute alternate URLs. | Retain the tested eight regional home/finder/directory sets. General guides and games remain global. No unreviewed French counterpart is declared. |
| [Multi-regional sites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites) | Explicit regional URLs and visitor-accessible switching are preferable to inferred IP redirects. | Country-specific URLs override saved preferences. Flags/full names accompany crawlable links; English stays separate. |
| [Faceted navigation](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/faceted-navigation) | Arbitrary combinations of filtering parameters can consume crawling without adding distinct value. | Food-brand shortcuts enhance one canonical library. Search results, dietary inputs and diary entries do not become separate public pages. |
| [Title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) | Titles should identify the page’s actual purpose clearly without long boilerplate or repeated keywords. | The grocery navigator names brands, countries, sources and portions; local guide titles follow exact serving questions, with source limitations near the answer. |

## Queries actually researched

The search tool does not expose a geographic locale. “Canada” or Canadian official domains constrained relevant queries; this is not a claim of a live Google Canada rank audit. Domain filters deliberately favoured primary sources. Observations below describe the returned page types, not demand or difficulty.

| Query family and current source | Useful page type observed | GetMacros decision |
|---|---|---|
| McDonald’s Canada nutrition — [official calculator](https://www.mcdonalds.com/ca/en-ca/about-our-food/nutrition-calculator.html) | Dynamic product selection; its empty calculator starts with zero, which is not an item’s nutrition. | Do not import those initial zeros. Canadian records require selected local serving values; source-interface failures remain explicit. |
| Tim Hortons breakfast protein — [official nutrition help](https://help.timhortons.ca/hc/en-ca/articles/35734906287643-Where-can-I-view-the-nutrition-and-allergen-information-for-Tim-Hortons-menu-items) | Official help sends visitors to item nutrition in the website/app. | Keep item/size/edition attached to tracked breakfast entries; do not substitute U.S. recipes. |
| Domino’s Canada pizza per slice — [official guide](https://www.dominos.ca/assets/build/market/CA/_en/pdf/Canadian-Nutrition-Guide-Final-Secure.pdf) | Ingredient tables distinguish size, crust, cheese quantities and fractions of a pizza. | Preserve the existing explicitly calculated small-slice builds and complete-order link. No generic “pizza slice” value. |
| Pizza Nova slice calories — [official nutrition](https://pizzanova.com/nutrition/) | In-store slices and made-to-order pizza size tables are different references. | Existing calorie-only slice records stay outside complete-macro filtering; do not fill missing protein, fat or carbs. |
| Canadian grocery labels / Kirkland / Great Value / President’s Choice — [official Costco Canada](https://www.costcobusinesscentre.ca/), [Walmart Canada](https://www.walmart.ca/), [President’s Choice](https://www.presidentschoice.ca/) | Product-specific packages and listings, not one label for an entire brand or a live warehouse inventory. | Add a navigator generated from actual country-attributed product records. Unsupported brands remain research gaps rather than nonfunctional brand names. |
| Canadian serving sizes and food labels — [Health Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-nutrition/nutrition-labelling/nutrition-facts-tables.html) | Label nutrients refer to the stated serving; household measures also have a metric equivalent. | Keep supported gram weights and named servings visible in review/comparison; mL never silently equals g. |

The two inherited source notes that read as editorial instructions were rewritten as concise visitor-facing limitations. The CAVA document’s uncertain connection to today’s menu and the Chick-fil-A sandwich’s unresolved sauce inclusion remain explicit. Nutrition values and source editions were not changed.
