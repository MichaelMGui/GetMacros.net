# Editorial review

Reviewed the extracted authored corpus in `copy-corpus.json`: headings, body paragraphs, list items, labels, options, table cells and captions. Metadata and dynamic tool messages were also checked in their source files. This was an editorial and consistency review, not a new medical review of every cited study or a fresh verification of all restaurant menus.

Changes include clearer meal composition, specific calculator tasks, shorter restaurant introductions, calmer article language, accurate 404 recovery links, explicit U.S. scope, and a clear distinction between unknown nutrition and zero. The search page gives concrete starting queries. Food-label and recipe examples explain portion assumptions. Publisher bylines remain GetMacros; no qualifications or expert reviewers were invented.

The final automated report contains 63 exact repeated blocks and four near-duplicate pairs. All candidates were reviewed. Retained repetitions are deliberate:

- Article titles repeated in directory and related links are navigation, not duplicate articles.
- Bylines, source names and original review dates remain consistent.
- Units, half-serving help, no-JavaScript instructions, missing-data statements, geographic scope, calculation methods and allergen limitations should not be rewritten differently on every page.
- The same named restaurant order can appear on the homepage, restaurant page and finder. Its composition must agree across those surfaces.
- The four near matches describe different Chipotle builds (rice or protein differences). Merging them would remove meaningful serving information. Two detections repeat because the source page includes the same build in its reference and finder content.

Generic repeated restaurant filler was removed. Necessary shared disclosures were retained. Desktop/mobile navigation uses one DOM structure. The separate static meal browser and interactive matches have distinct tasks and disclosure, rather than two simultaneously expanded copies of the same results.

No design-only blanket update to article review dates was made. The serving-size article's new worked example has a substantive September 23 update. Existing restaurant verification dates were preserved, not represented as newly checked today.
