# Restaurant search research and implemented page decisions

Researched October 12, 2026 (client timezone: America/Toronto). No current authorized Search Console export, analytics export, keyword-volume tool or rank tracker was available to this subtask. The observations below are qualitative search results, not measured demand, rankings, traffic, CTR or revenue forecasts. Queries include Canada explicitly; the search tool's physical locale is not independently controlled or confirmed.

## Current primary guidance inspected

| Source | Finding applied in this module |
|---|---|
| [Google: helpful, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) | A source table alone needs added practical value. Six guides include serving comparisons, exact arithmetic and material limitations, rather than pages for each product/nutrient keyword. Dates distinguish retrieval, source edition and meaningful table review. No fake authors, qualifications or firsthand restaurant testing. |
| [Google: spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) | No doorway pages for every chain × nutrient × size. No thin indexed pages for each record, hidden keywords, purchased links or automated Google-query traffic. Unsupported Canadian chains live as clearly labelled directory entries rather than fabricated nutrition articles. |
| [Google: managing multi-regional sites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites) | Real Canadian URLs use local facts, explicit country links and independent canonicals. No IP redirect and no U.S. source values presented as Canadian. Existing U.S. URLs remain. |
| [Google: localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions) | The six new chain guides are distinct regional sources, not automatically language alternates of unrelated U.S. articles. English pages are not marked reviewed French. Root's genuinely equivalent country entry points retain reciprocal regional annotations. |
| [Google: faceted navigation](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/faceted-navigation) | Finder refinements and private food searches do not become an indexed page for every filter/number. Guides are the curated canonical landing pages; query parameters drive actual tools. |
| [Google: title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) | Each guide has a descriptive chain + Canada + serving/task title and one useful visible heading, without stuffing every macro into repeated headings. The shared renderer owns final metadata/canonical/structured breadcrumbs. |

## Actual query observations

| `Tim Hortons breakfast protein` | [Official omelette-bite news](https://www.news.timhortons.ca/en/articles/omelette-bites), [GripRoom breakfast article](https://www.griproom.com/fun/best-tim-hortons-breakfast-for-protein), [Ripped Restaurants breakfast list](https://rippedrestaurants.com/guides/best-breakfast-foods-at-tim-hortons), and [existing GetMacros Canadian guide](https://getmacros.net/ca/en/restaurants/tim-hortons/). | Keep existing local URL and source selection. | `Pizza Nova calories per slice` | Search shows sparse official coverage and third-party estimates/menus; [official source](https://pizzanova.com/nutrition) needed ordinary browser inspection. | Answer exact in-store slice calories near the top. | `Domino's Canada nutrition small pizza` | [Official Canadian PDF](https://www.dominos.ca/assets/build/market/CA/_en/pdf/Canadian-Nutrition-Guide-Final-Secure.pdf), a [Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/862849004/Canada-nutrition-guide), [MyNetDiary small-pizza entry](https://www.mynetdiary.com/food/calories-in-canadian-small-pizza-by-domino-s-ca-of-pizza-60237620-0.html), and U.K./Ireland guides with different bases. | The important gap is denominator/cheese-rule clarity. | `Subway Canada Fresh Fit nutrition protein` | [Official 2026 announcement](https://newsroom.subway.com/2026-01-05-Subway-R-Canada-Powers-Up-2026-with-Its-New-Fresh-Fit-Menu-Five-Protein-Packed-Sandwiches-Under-500-Calories), [official Rotisserie recipe page](https://www.subway.com/en-ca/restaurant/71870-0/customizer/productsummary/15328/category/sandwiches), [SubwayMacros local tables](https://www.subwaymacros.com/ca/subs), and third-party calculators. | Distinguish same-name protein in Classics, Fresh Fit and wrap builds. New guide compares exact portions, shows the explicit footlong rule and links the country finder and private log. | `Booster Juice protein smoothie calories regular snack` | Official September guide, [Booster Juice FAQ](https://boosterjuice.com/en-ca/a/faq), and local menu pages such as [Fanshawe Student Union](https://www.fsu.ca/booster-juice-menu). Other results include unrelated Boost Juice overseas sources. | Show named regular/snack recipes and protein quantities. Do not conflate Booster Juice Canada with Boost Juice in Australia/NZ/UK. Volume, total sugar and exact size limitations are part of the new guide. |
| `Mary Brown's Big Mary calories small taters` | [Official Canadian menu](https://marybrowns.com/menu/) and price/estimate sites with divergent serving figures. Current menu links the July 2026 nutrition file. | A combo range is not one exact order. New guide sums one source sandwich and one small side, excludes drinks, keeps source discrepancies visible and withholds ambiguous bucket totals. |
| `Pizza Pizza small pizza calories per slice` | [Official small-pizza source](https://www.pizzapizza.ca/about-us/nutrition/small-pizza/) directly answers size/slices, alongside generic third-party pizza logging pages. | Add accurate one/two/six-slice arithmetic and clear missing/contradictory fields. A separate page for every small pizza flavour would be unnecessary; one substantial guide with supported finder records serves the task. |

The competing page observations above describe returned page types and inspected visible content/search excerpts. They do not validate competitors' nutrient values, estimate their traffic, establish their Google rank, or prove keyword search volume.

## Implemented query-to-page map

Six guide specs are generated by `tools/build_master_restaurants.py`, each using the shared site renderer. Root integrates the new guides into the Canadian restaurant directory, sitemap, search and source-aware finder. The quantitative snapshots are in `tools/master_release/restaurant_records.json`. Tool refinements remain non-indexable states of existing tools.

| Canonical route | Main user intent | Original useful answer | Relevant next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/ca/en/restaurants/subway/` | Compare Canadian standard sub/wrap/salad/breakfast nutrition | Same protein across three real builds; explicit footlong doubling; source inclusion notes | Canada finder, portion logging, Canadian restaurant directory |
| `/ca/en/restaurants/domino-s/` | Calories/macros for a Canadian small hand-tossed slice | Exact crust/sauce/cheese/topping sum; cheese-only versus topping quantity; slice denominator | Exact-order builder, Canada finder, portion logging |
| `/ca/en/restaurants/mary-brown-s/` | Compare Canadian chicken, Big Mary and sides | Single-piece/side comparison; a 1,066 kcal exact two-component order; unresolved bucket restrictions | Canada finder, portion logging |
| `/ca/en/restaurants/booster-juice/` | Smoothie protein by recipe and size | Actual regular/snack serving values; total sugar versus added-sugar unknown; volume versus mass | Country drink/finder selection and portion logging |
| `/ca/en/restaurants/pizza-pizza/` | Small Canadian pizza calories per slice | Exact 10-inch/six-slice arithmetic; sat-fat contradictions stay unknown | Canada finder, portion logging |
| `/ca/en/restaurants/pizza-nova/` | Official Canadian in-store slice calories | Exact calorie-only entries; unknown macros and signature denominator remain explicit | Log the calorie entry or choose complete Canadian records elsewhere |

No indexing or traffic gain is claimed by this document. Publication, sitemap submission, crawling, indexing and clicks require separate production/Search Console evidence.
