Zizzi Italian Restaurants

It all started in Chiswick, with a little neighbourhood Italian restaurant, one pizza oven and a few glasses of wine. Fast forward 27 years, and we’ve got over 130 Italian restaurants. Sounds like a lot now we’ve written it down. We’re still doing things our way. Bowlfuls of pasta, pizzas with light, crispy bases, generous portions and food that makes you go “oooooo” when it hits the table. And dessert? Always. Life’s too short to skip the sweet stuff. Come hungry. Leave happy.

DUBLIN

May the road rise to meet you, but may it also have a few detours for pizza along the way.

We've three Italian restaurants in Dublin - Dundrum Town Centre, Liffey Valley Shopping Centre, and Suffolk Street. We’ll take care of you whether you're here for work, the sights, or just the craic.

Dine-in or Italian takeaway - whatever and wherever you're eating in Dublin. Our vibe is as easygoing as our menu. Think hand-stretched Rustica pizzas. Fresh pasta. The sweetest cocktails and desserts. And no compromises in our vegan pizza and non-gluten pasta.

Italians love a party. Your next special occasion in Dublin is in safe hands with our restaurants. We've hosted hundreds of birthday parties, anniversaries, hen parties, baby showers and private dining bookings.
