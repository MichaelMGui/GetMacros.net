OUR STORY

OUR STORY

THE HERITAGE

What began in a small north London shop in 1975 has grown into one of Britain’s best-loved bakeries. Founded by Peter Wenzel and still proudly run by the Wenzel’s family today, our bakery is built on a deep-rooted passion for quality, freshness and community. Whether you’re here for a classic favourite or something new, we’re proud to offer freshly baked goods that bring our whole community together. For generations, we’ve believed in making it all ourselves. We knead the dough, we fill the tarts, we bake it all, every day.
