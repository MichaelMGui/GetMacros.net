We believe that great food starts with great baking. Our bread, cakes, and sandwiches are freshly made every night by our expert team of bakers in our Watford bakery, before being delivered to stores early each morning. In-store, we freshly fill baguettes, prepare sandwiches and heat up favourite snacks and pastries throughout the day, so there’s always something fresh ready to enjoy.

Our menu brings together bakery favourites made for breakfast, lunch and everything in between. From freshly baked bread and filled baguettes to hot food, cakes, pastries and sweet treats, our range is built around freshness, convenience and quality. Coffee and quick bites for busy mornings sit alongside hearty lunches, comforting savoury options and afternoon treats.

As a proudly family-run business with over 50 years of baking heritage, Wenzels Bakery continues to serve local communities with freshly prepared food at great value. Searching for a bakery near you should mean finding quality products, friendly service, and plenty of choice all in one place. And that’s exactly what Wenzel’s the Bakers is all about.

From the first coffee of the morning to an afternoon sweet treat, the Wenzels menu is filled with freshly prepared favourites designed to leave you smiling and satisfied.
