Important Notice about our Coconut Drink

From the 23rd March our Coconut drink will now contain Soya.

If you have a Soya allergy or prefer to avoid it, please keep this in mind when choosing any beverage made with Coconut drink, either in-store, on the Starbucks® UK app or through your delivery provider.

If you have any questions, please check our Nutrition & Allergen booklet above, or remember to speak with our friendly baristas, they are always happy to help recommend an alternative that suits your needs or preferences.

Allergen Information

Our beverages and food items are prepared where allergens are handled and equipment is shared. We cannot guarantee that any items are allergen free. 

When you select your food on our menu page, the below allergen icons will appear underneath the nutrition information table to inform you of the allergens present in your food. There will be no allergen information displayed for beverages, so always check the Nutrition & Allergen booklets for all beverage allergen information including customisations, prior to ordering.

  • Celery
  • Mustard
  • Cereals containing gluten (Wheat, Rye, Barley, Oat, Spelt and Kamut)
  • Nuts (Almond, Hazelnut, Walnut, Brazil nut, Cashew, Pecan, Pistachio and Macadamia nut)
  • Crustaceans
  • Peanuts
  • Eggs
  • Sesame
  • Fish
  • Soya
  • Lupin
  • Sulphur dioxide and sulphites
  • Milk
  • Molluscs
