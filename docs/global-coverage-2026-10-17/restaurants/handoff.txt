Source boundaries
  This batch is not a complete current-menu or every-branch availability claim.
  Drink, dessert, side and ingredient records are real source rows. Components
  stay outside macro meal matching. Missing fibre, sugar or saturated fat is null.
  Source less-than values retain nutrientBounds; a bound is not exact zero or its
  exact upper limit. Bound-dependent macro rows are excluded from matching.
  KFC UK&I sodium is salt(g) × 1000 ÷ 2.5. MOS Japan directly published sodium
  takes precedence over a conversion from its rounded salt equivalent.
  Source editions, download dates and review dates are separate fields.
  Web-extracted numeric files are explicitly identified as web captures; their
  hashes do not pretend to identify original PDF or HTML bytes.
