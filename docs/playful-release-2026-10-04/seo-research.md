# Search research — 4 October 2026

No Search Console account or export was connected for this release. Earlier photos are historical observations, not a current dataset. No volumes, difficulty estimates, projected rankings or revenue figures are inferred here.

## Guidance actually inspected

| Source | Applied decision |
|---|---|
| [Google: helpful, reliable content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) | Require a distinct reader question, original worked example or analysis, useful sources and an editorial check. Retain uncertainty and substantive verification dates. Publication count alone is not a quality test. |
| [Google: spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) | Do not publish keyword permutations, title-swapped articles or thin game shells to inflate coverage. Drafts and blocked items stay outside public indexes. |
| [Google: JavaScript SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics) | Preserve crawlable static links and substantive initial HTML. Games progressively enhance visible objectives, instructions and source boundaries. |
| [Google: faceted navigation](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/faceted-navigation) | Filter combinations and puzzle seeds are interface states, not separate sitemap pages. Retain a deliberate base canonical and established URLs. |
| [Google: page experience](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/page-experience) | Test actual interactions, mobile layout and performance. No promise of AdSense approval or a universal “zero” HTML-speed requirement. Lab checks are reported separately from field data. |
| [Google: structured-data introduction](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data) | Schema describes visible content only; no fabricated ratings, expert reviews, medical credentials or restaurant recipes. |
| [Google: Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) | Keep source-linked, descriptive page titles, crawlable links and technically accessible pages. |

## Query observations

These were live qualitative search inspections, not traffic measurements. Search locale was not explicitly pinned; the product's restaurant data is U.S.-specific.

| Query | Observation | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| high protein fast food meals under 600 calories restaurant nutrition comparison | Results commonly use ranked order lists and calorie/protein thresholds. Some distinguish complete orders; others blur custom combinations, main items and meals. [One observed result type](https://nutricalculators.com/highest-protein-fast-food-under-600-calories/). Its nutrition claims were not adopted as data. | Keep exact composition, source date and serving beside results; use the verified official dataset instead of copying rankings. Numeric limits remain explicit and are never silently relaxed. |
| nutrition serving size calculator compare food labels per 100 grams | Search surfaces practical label tools and public-health material explaining serving versus equal-weight comparisons. [Queensland Health's label guide](https://www.health.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0027/441774/reading-food-labels.pdf) illustrates this intent. | Task-led calculator links, a clearly illustrative live equal-portion example, and individual articles addressing specific accounting mistakes. Regional label formats stay explicit. |

The new editorial source manifest records each article's primary intent, supporting sources, unique utility and related tool. That is the query-to-page map; not every intent is claimed to be a separately researched keyword.
