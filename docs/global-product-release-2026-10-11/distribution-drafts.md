# Prepared distribution — not sent or posted

All claims below point to actual source-backed tools. Verify the final live URLs before use. No traffic predictions, paid placements or health promises are included.

## Short video scripts

1. **Raw or cooked?** Show two 100 g chicken reference profiles. “The weight you use matters. Choose the profile that matches when you weighed your chicken.” Show the source edition and the raw/cooked guide. Explain that these are different reference profiles, not a test of the same breast before and after cooking.
2. **The oil you added.** Show a teaspoon and tablespoon using the source’s 4.5 g and 13.5 g measures. “A little oil can mean different amounts. Review the portion, then add it to your notebook.” Show manual review; never imply the camera measured the oil.
3. **Same restaurant, different country.** Show the UK and New Zealand Subway builds with both source editions and serving weights. “Familiar names don’t establish identical recipes. Compare the country, portion and documented components.” End on the country comparison tool.
4. **A notebook that stays with you.** Search a food, check its preparation and portion, save it, then show edit and export. “Stored in this browser. Export a backup before clearing site data.” Do not promise cloud sync or a connected AI service.

## Newsletter drafts

**Subject: Your food notebook is ready**

Search everyday food profiles, review the portion and keep a private diary in your browser. Recipes retain their ingredient snapshots, and incomplete nutrients stay visibly incomplete. Start at https://getmacros.net/food-diary.html. Export a backup if you want to move devices. Photo and voice analysis is awaiting backend setup; manual logging already works.

**Subject: Check the country before the calories**

GetMacros now separates menu sources for eight countries. The ingredient comparator keeps country, serving and document edition together, including the limits of partial ingredient lists. Start at https://getmacros.net/world-restaurant-menus.html. These are tracked selections, not a live full-menu audit.
