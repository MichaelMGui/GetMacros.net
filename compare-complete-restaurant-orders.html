Eating out

Make sure the two numbers describe comparable orders

An order checklist and worked comparison show why an entrée-only figure and a meal with sides can give a misleading ranking.

In this guide
How the apparent winner can changeA five-line comparison worksheetUse the result that answers your question

By GetMacros · Published October 3, 2026

Compare the orders you would actually buy. A lower number for an entrée alone is not a fair comparison with an entrée, side and drink. Keep the included items next to the nutrition values.

FDA’s menu guide explains that meals with choices may show multiple calorie values or ranges. A range does not identify your exact combination by itself.

How the apparent winner can change

These invented orders demonstrate a comparison problem; they are not restaurant data.

ComponentOrder AOrder B
Entrée420 calories510 calories
Chosen side180 calories80 calories
Chosen drink120 calories0 calories
Complete order720 calories590 calories

Entrée A is 90 calories lower than entrée B. But complete order A is 130 calories higher than complete order B. Neither calculation is wrong; they answer different questions.

A five-line comparison worksheet

  1. Menu region: use the same country’s menu for each source.
  2. Size: identify six-inch versus footlong, regular versus large, or one versus two pieces.
  3. Included items: list sides, sauce, dressing, toppings and drinks.
  4. Preparation: check standard versus modified orders and how substitutions were verified.
  5. Nutrition coverage: retain unknown fields instead of filling them with zero.

For a dish intended for sharing, state whether the figure describes the whole dish or your estimated share. Do not divide a layered or uneven dish into exact equal nutrition portions unless that assumption is reasonable and visible.

Use the result that answers your question

Whole-order calories describe the energy in the listed combination. Protein per 100 calories describes a ratio. Protein grams describe an amount. A meal can lead on one measure and trail on another, so keep both the absolute values and the comparison basis visible.

Compare tracked orders side by side. Use the source link to verify current portions and modifications; a hypothetical subtraction is not an official customized order.
