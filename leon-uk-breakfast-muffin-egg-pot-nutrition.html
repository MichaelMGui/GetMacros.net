LEON UK breakfast: muffins, egg pot and sando compared

Published October 18, 2026 · United Kingdom source servings

LEON’s UK Sausage & Egg Muffin lists 454 kcal and 25 g protein. The Smoked Salmon & Avocado Egg Pot lists 211 kcal and 13 g protein. The pot is a smaller, different recipe; it is not the same breakfast with the bread removed.

Compare the whole breakfast

Use one named portion when deciding what to order. The egg pot weighs 145 g in the source, the two savoury muffins 217–224 g, and the halloumi sando 271 g. A lower number per portion can partly reflect a smaller amount of food.

Two muffins, a small calorie difference

The Bacon & Egg Muffin has 431 kcal and 24 g protein. The Sausage & Egg Muffin has 23 kcal more and 1 g more protein. Those differences are small compared with the change from either muffin to the egg pot or the larger sando.

Salt is a separate comparison: LEON lists 3.7 g for the bacon muffin and 2.1 g for the sausage muffin. The lower-calorie muffin therefore does not have the lower salt figure. Neither recipe is a universal winner; compare the fields you actually care about.

Compare the two muffins by their published portions

What changes with an egg pot?

The salmon and avocado pot has 243 fewer kcal and 12 g less protein than the sausage-and-egg muffin. It also lists 2.5 g carbohydrate and 16 g fat. This is useful if you want a smaller breakfast, but it does not tell us how full you will feel or what else you should eat.

The recipes contain different ingredients and amounts. Do not subtract the pot’s values from a muffin to estimate the nutrition of bread, sauce or an egg. Log the named item that you actually ordered; add a coffee, side or extra separately using its own serving.

When you want the larger option

The Halloumi, Egg & Avo Sando lists 610 kcal and 31 g protein, with 41 g carbohydrate and 35 g fat. Compared with the sausage muffin, that is 156 more kcal for 6 g more protein. Its 271 g serving and different filling are part of the choice, not an unexplained upgrade.

LEON’s menu groups these items under breakfast, shown as until 11 am in the source read on 18 October 2026. Check your branch: a source-listed breakfast is not a promise that every restaurant stocks it or serves it at every hour.
