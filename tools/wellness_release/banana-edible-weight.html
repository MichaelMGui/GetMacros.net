Weigh the banana without its peel when using an edible raw banana entry. The USDA SR Legacy reference gives 89 calories per 100 g of the raw edible food. Its medium portion is 118 g, which works out to about 105 calories.

When a yield is useful

The USDA Food Buying Guide lists about 0.64 lb prepared banana slices per pound purchased for listed whole banana sizes. The second row uses that ratio for an illustration. It does not mean every individual fruit is exactly 64% flesh. For fruit you can weigh after peeling, use the first method.

The same principle holds for a peel, pit or trim you discard. Choose a record and a weight that both describe the food you actually ate. The Nutrient Density Explorer lists the USDA medium banana portion beside the value per 100 g.
