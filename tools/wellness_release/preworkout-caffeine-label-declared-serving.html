Start with the serving definition in Supplement Facts. Then look for a named amount of caffeine. The U.S. C4 Ultimate page identifies a 300 mg caffeine amount for the advertised formula and separately names other ingredients. The total weight of a combined blend cannot stand in for the caffeine inside it.

Which label line to use
What you seeHow to read it
A named caffeine amountKeep the stated amount with the exact serving, market and formulation.
A proprietary blend with a total weight onlyThe listed amount can refer to the blend. Keep a missing caffeine amount unknown unless the manufacturer verifies it separately.
A claim on the container frontConfirm its units and serving size against the exact version’s label.

Do not compare a familiar product name across versions

Formulations can change. A similarly named or flavored product from another country might use a different serving or caffeine declaration. If you’re comparing products, keep each current label and the amount actually consumed with the comparison. Caffeine from a separate drink belongs on its own line.
