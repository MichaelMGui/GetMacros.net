Skin hydration probes pick up electrical properties related to water in the outer layer of the skin. Different devices can emphasize different depths or respond differently at very dry and very moist sites. When a paper reports a difference in “hydration units,” look for the device and method before comparing it with another paper.

What to copy out of the methods

Write down the probe model, site on the body, interval after washing or product application, and time since baseline. Compare the same zone, on the same device, over the same interval. If two papers use different devices, keep their changes in their own scale even when both show an increase.
