Which column answers your question?

If you want to know what was in the 12-piece box, read 38 g or 40 g from the total column. If you want to compare how much protein each option lists relative to its energy, divide grams by calories and multiply by 100. For example, 38 ÷ 200 × 100 is 19.0. That result expresses a ratio. It does not say the restaurant sells an exact 100-calorie nugget serving.

Compare the rest of the order separately

In the restaurant’s guide, the 12 grilled pieces weigh 142 g and the 12 breaded pieces weigh 170 g. Same piece count is not the same weight. Each figure describes the listed nuggets alone. If you order a side, sauce or drink, add its listed portion once and calculate any ratio from the new protein and calorie totals. Rounding two source numbers can create a small apparent difference, so a tenth of a gram in the ratio is not evidence of a meaningful difference in the food.
