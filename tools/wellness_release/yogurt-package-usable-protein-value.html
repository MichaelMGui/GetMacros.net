A larger tub can give you more protein per dollar if you eat enough of it. The U.S. Chobani plain nonfat tub shows 16 g protein per listed serving. The U.S. Oikos Triple Zero Vanilla cup shows 15 g in one 5.3 oz cup. Start with those quantities, then count the servings you expect to eat.

At $6, the tub needs to provide 60 g of protein eaten to match the cup’s 10 g per dollar in this example. That is 3.75 of the displayed Chobani servings. If the actual prices change, so does the comparison. This calculation also does not compare the taste or other nutrition of plain and vanilla yogurt.
