Replacing half of a 200 g cooked beef portion with boiled lentils changes the USDA-based estimate from 460 to 346 kcal. Protein falls from about 57 to 37 g, and the lentils add almost 8 g of fiber. The comparison keeps the weight the same.

The blend loses about 114 kcal and 19.4 g protein and gains 7.9 g fiber across the 200 g base. If two bowls share it evenly, that works out to roughly −57 kcal, −9.7 g protein and +4 g fiber per bowl. That calculation assumes each bowl contains the same beef-to-lentil ratio.
