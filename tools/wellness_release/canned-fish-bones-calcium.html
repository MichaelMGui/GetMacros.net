In USDA reference data, 85 g of canned pink salmon listed as drained solids provides about 241 mg calcium. The separate entry for drained solids without skin and bones gives about 51 mg at the same weight. Oil-packed Atlantic sardines with bones have about 325 mg in an 85 g drained portion.

Match the condition before multiplying

The two salmon records are reference foods, not a paired trial on the same tin. Their difference therefore should not be treated as the exact effect of taking out bones. If you remove bones and skin, choose a matching entry when available. Weigh the drained portion after removing what you leave behind.

The sardine value applies to the bone-in reference. The table cannot tell you the calcium left in this fish after you remove its bones. Check a boneless product label if that is what you used. Open the Nutrient Density Explorer to inspect the bone-in sardine record.
