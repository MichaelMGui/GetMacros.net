Check whether the measure is dry or cooked

Cooked oatmeal is another comparison point, at 0.7 mg in half a cup in the same source. These halves refer to cooked food. A dry measure has a different mass and cannot be substituted for a cooked amount simply because both say half a cup.

The rice records differ in grain length as well as milling. They support a comparison between these two entries, rather than a universal estimate of the effect of milling.
