Use the package that matches the bowl

Multiply the tub’s printed amounts by 150 ÷ 170. That gives about 1.9 g less protein and about 11 fewer calories at the matched weight. The comparison changes because one label calls a larger amount a serving. The amounts come from rounded packaging, so the decimals show the calculation rather than lab precision.

For a whole single-serve cup, use that product’s own label. For a scoop from a tub, weigh it and scale the tub’s figure. Vanilla, sweetened and whole-milk versions need their own values. To compare these numbers with a food from a recipe, start with the protein and fiber comparison.
