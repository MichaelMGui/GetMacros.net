A hair shaft’s width and its growth rate are different measurements. A calibrated image can show apparent width at one time. To estimate growth in millimeters per day, researchers need a length change over a known time interval.

Count the observations correctly

A 2026 proof-of-concept report measured 20 shafts from one trichoscopic image. That is 20 shaft observations and one person. A separate method study found that magnification affected which hairs the photos could detect. Ask how the images were scaled, when they were taken, and how many people were measured.
