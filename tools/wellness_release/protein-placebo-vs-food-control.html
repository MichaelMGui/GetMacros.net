Replacing a carbohydrate placebo with protein changes both the source and the protein amount in the treatment. Giving the same protein amount from several foods asks a narrower question about sources. Consider the control before deciding what an exercise study actually tested.

Follow the comparison and the outcome

A calorie-matched placebo helps control the energy of the study products. It does not supply the same protein. A protein-matched trial may still supply foods with different carbohydrate, fat and other properties. The short-term study reports protein turnover measurements. A long-term change in strength or size would need a separate follow-up.

Ask what remains different between conditions, then phrase the result in that scope. To compare two actual products by weight, see the NOW whey and soy isolate label example.
