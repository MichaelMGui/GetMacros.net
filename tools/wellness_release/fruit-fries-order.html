Adding a small fruit cup to U.S. Chick-fil-A 8-count breaded nuggets gives a calculated 310 kcal. Adding small waffle fries to the same nuggets gives 570 kcal. The difference is 260 kcal. The fries version has more listed fiber, as well as more sodium, in these exact portions.

Keep the side size in view

Small is the restaurant’s size name for both sides. It does not indicate the same weight. The fruit cup is 11 g heavier in the guide, with fewer calories per portion. The fries provide an additional 2 g fiber and 190 mg sodium compared with the fruit cup. If you change to a larger size, use that size’s own published numbers.
