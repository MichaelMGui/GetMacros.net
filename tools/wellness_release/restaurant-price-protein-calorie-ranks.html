Calculate the price that would tie the result

If the Original costs $6.00, multiply $6.00 by 28 ÷ 29. The grilled sandwich ties its protein per dollar at about $5.79 under the same fee treatment. That calculation uses the two sandwich values only. If you buy a complete order, compare the order’s full price and protein together.
