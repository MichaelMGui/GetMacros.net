The 2025 randomized trial that measured hair directly did not find a significant difference between creatine monohydrate and placebo on its hair measures over 12 weeks. The result is specific to that trial’s group and follow-up. It cannot rule out every possible effect in every person.

Read the sample and the time window

The newer study recruited 45 men aged 18 to 40 and 38 completed it. The investigators included measurements such as hair density and cumulative thickness. That gets closer to what the headline asks than a measurement of a hormone alone. It is still a modest sample with a 12-week follow-up. The result cannot settle effects over years, in different groups, or for other supplement blends.

When evaluating the next claim, check whether the researchers measured hair or cited a mechanism that might affect it. Then ask who was studied and for how long. A sudden change in hair or scalp symptoms is a separate clinical question with multiple possible causes.
