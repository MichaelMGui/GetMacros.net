A 60 g Quest Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough bar lists 21 g protein. The 50 g Pure Protein Chewy Chocolate Chip bar lists 20 g. The slightly heavier bar provides one more gram per bar, while the smaller bar has a larger share of its weight as protein.

Which percentage is it?

Divide protein grams by the bar’s weight, then multiply by 100. That shows mass fraction. The percent Daily Value on the Nutrition Facts label uses a daily reference and may incorporate protein quality rules. It is a different comparison. A mass fraction also cannot tell you about sugar, energy, ingredients, price or taste.

If you normally eat the whole bar, the per-bar figure answers how much protein you logged. To compare equal 50 g portions, scale the 60 g Quest example to about 17.5 g protein.
