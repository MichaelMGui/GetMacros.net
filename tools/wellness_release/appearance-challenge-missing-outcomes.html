Progress posts usually show people who kept posting and images the organizer chose to share. If the montage leaves out people who stopped, ordinary fluctuations or unremarkable outcomes, it cannot tell you how often the promised result happened across everyone who started.

Check how success was defined

Ofcom’s research describes experiences with online self-improvement and appearance content. A controlled study would separately disclose who entered, what counted as an outcome and how missing results were handled. A challenge reel generally cannot answer those questions from the images alone. Ordinary variation does not acquire medical meaning because a creator labels it failure.
