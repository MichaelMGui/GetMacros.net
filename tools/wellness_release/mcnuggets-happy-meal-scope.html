McDonald’s U.S. lists 170 calories for four Chicken McNuggets by themselves. The chain gives 395 calories for its four-piece Happy Meal with kids’ fries, apple slices and 1% milk. Both figures refer to a four-piece main. They differ because the second total includes three other items.

Choose either the published total or the components

If you ate the published configuration, use 395 for the food and drink listed. Adding the nuggets again would give 565 and would count the main twice. If you changed the drink or left part of the sides, keep the components you actually ate and use their separately published portions. The 225-calorie difference does not tell you how much of the difference came from the milk by itself.

Check the exact fries and drink

The published figure names kids’ fries, so a small fries figure belongs to another portion. Likewise, an alternate drink can have different nutrition. If you have a sauce, check whether it is included in the source you are using. The chain’s 395-calorie example lists the main, sides and drink shown above.
