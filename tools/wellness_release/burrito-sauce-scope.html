Start with the exact version listed. Taco Bell describes Red Sauce as a filling in its standard U.S. Bean Burrito. Chick-fil-A describes a chicken Hash Brown Scramble Burrito with Jalapeño Salsa and tells customers to check the item’s Included section for the nutrition scope. A sauce appearing beside a burrito in a photo does not answer whether it was counted.

If you leave a sauce out, prefer a source-supported without-sauce version. Without a matching amount or removal option, the adjusted total remains an estimate. Do not subtract a whole packet’s value from a burrito that already excludes it.
