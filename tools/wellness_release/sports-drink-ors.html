A sports drink and an oral rehydration solution can both contain electrolytes. Oral rehydration solution, or ORS, has a clinical purpose related to replacing fluid and electrolytes lost in diarrhoeal illness. A sports drink is a different product category. Having some of the same ingredients does not make two formulations interchangeable.

Read the conditions alongside the result

The 72 men started out hydrated. The trial gave a litre over 30 minutes and followed fluid outcomes for a few hours. It was a comparison of short-term retention under defined conditions, rather than a test of sports performance or treating an illness. In a different four-beverage study of 31 athletes, one sports drink produced lower four-hour urine output than water while the other did not show the same result. “Sports drink” alone does not specify which beverage or result a claim is based on.

When reading a claim, identify the exact beverage, the starting hydration state and what the researchers measured. If you want to compare sodium on everyday drink labels, use the food and drink comparisons and match the amount you actually drink.
