Pizza Pizza lists 210 calories for a slice of its small pepperoni pizza and for a slice of its medium pepperoni pizza. Its small slice represents one sixth of a 10-inch pie. Its medium slice represents one eighth of a 12-inch pie. The same number per slice therefore gives different totals for the same fraction of each pizza.
