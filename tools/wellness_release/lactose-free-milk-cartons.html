How lactose-free milk can still show sugar

Organic Valley lists lactase in the lactose-free half-gallon. The enzyme breaks lactose into glucose and galactose. Those smaller sugars still count in total sugars. Both Organic Valley panels list 0 g added sugar. Total sugar does not tell you how much of it is left in the form of lactose.

Keep the exact package in the comparison

A half-cup pour provides 4 g protein from either of the Organic Valley half-gallons or about 6.5 g from the protein-enriched product, using the published values. Organic Valley’s page also shows an 8 g figure for the regular gallon and a 9 g figure for the regular quart, both per cup. We used the matched half-gallons and did not carry the quart figure across the line. If you’re saving a food in your diary, use the brand, variant and serving on the carton you actually have.
