The Santa Cruz Paleo Mango tub page describes 1,000 mg sodium per scoop in its product copy and marketing graphic. The separate single-stick product shown on the same page is listed at 800 mg per stick. Those are observed figures for the specific formats shown. They do not establish the amount in another flavor, a different label edition, or every product associated with Santa Cruz Medicinals.

A concentration example

In a fictional example, the same 300 mg of sodium is present when the whole serving is dissolved in 500 mL or 750 mL. The first gives 60 mg per 100 mL. The second gives 40 mg per 100 mL. The amounts in this example illustrate a denominator; they are unrelated to the brand’s preparation directions.

A mineral amount is separate from a hydration result

A primary beverage study defined a short-term hydration measure relative to still water using urine output and fluid retention. The study’s conditions, products and time points determine what its result can support. It did not test the Santa Cruz Paleo formulation described here. When a product page makes a broad claim, look for an outcome test of that product in the setting being described, and use the actual package to confirm its amounts.
