At 100 g, the USDA fresh yellow peach entry has about 8.4 g total sugars. Heavy-syrup canned peach solids and liquids have 18.6 g, and the drained heavy-syrup peach entry has 14.7 g. Choose the record that describes what you ate.

What changes in a 150 g bowl?

At that weight, the examples contain about 12.6, 27.9 or 22.1 g of total sugars, respectively. Drained means the liquid was removed before weighing the serving. You still need the drained record or label. The two canned entries are independent, so their difference does not tell you how much added sugar you removed from a specific can.

Use the package’s “Includes Added Sugars” line to answer the added-sugar question. Total sugar includes sugars in the fruit and any added sugars in the product. If the package specifies juice, light syrup, or a different preparation, use that product’s own label instead. See how to pick the serving or package column.
