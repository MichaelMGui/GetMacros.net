Weigh the edible cooked meat after carving. The USDA original-seasoning rotisserie reference gives 28 g protein per 100 g of breast meat and 24.06 g per 100 g of thigh meat. The entries describe meat only, so compare them with portions weighed without the skin or bones.

Keep the record matched to what you eat

The weight on a whole bird’s label can include bones and other parts you leave behind. If you log only the breast you carved, use only the first line. If you eat the skin, use a matching meat-and-skin or separate skin record for the amount consumed. The exact store seasoning and preparation may differ from these generic U.S. references.

For a serving made up of several cuts, sum the cuts rather than assuming the entire plate behaves like breast meat. Log your measured portions in the diary.
