One USDA reference cup of boiled white beans provides about 17.4 g protein. A cup of canned chickpeas that have been drained and rinsed provides about 10.7 g. The white bean cup weighs more, and the white beans in these entries also contain more protein at an equal weight.

With 160 g of either bean in the pot, the per-bowl estimates are about 3.9 g and 2.8 g. Replacing a cup changes both the weight and the nutrient concentration, so it answers a different question. These are estimates from two prepared entries. For a can packed in liquid, look for the nutrition data that match drained solids.

The calculation assumes four servings with an equal share of the beans. Stir and divide the solids evenly if the portions need to match. Add broth, vegetables and other ingredients to estimate the whole bowl in the food diary.
