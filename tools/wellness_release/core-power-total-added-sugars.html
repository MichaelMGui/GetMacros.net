The U.S. 26 g Core Power chocolate shake lists 5 g total sugars and 0 g added sugars per bottle. Total sugars includes sugars already present in the ingredients and any added sugars reported under FDA rules. The word “includes” on the label tells you to read the added-sugar line as part of the total.
