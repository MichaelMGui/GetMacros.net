Two small crossover trials compared dinner around one hour and five hours before bedtime. The one-night laboratory study reported no difference in sleep architecture. A 2026 study in healthy young women found shorter sleep and more time awake after sleep onset at the end of several days of later dinners.

Check the outcome and the kind of meal

A fixed lab bedtime and a habitual bedtime in daily life are different settings. Count the nights of the intervention and check whether the researchers cared about duration, awakenings, stage distribution or a metabolic measure. In each study the meals were standardized between timing conditions. The findings do not compare snacks with complete dinners.
