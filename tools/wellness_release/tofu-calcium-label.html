Convert both labels to a portion you would eat

Say your recipe uses 80 g. Multiply the 100 g figures by 0.8 and the examples give about 161 mg and 546 mg, respectively. For a labeled portion of a different weight, multiply the calcium per portion by 80 and divide by its listed grams.

The pair also differs in protein and preparation. It cannot isolate the effect of adding a particular coagulant. The USDA’s technical review documents several commercial coagulants and tofu varieties. Compare products using the package’s own calcium figure when it is available. For a repeat recipe, save the exact tofu and serving in the food diary so a brand switch does not carry over the old numbers by accident.
