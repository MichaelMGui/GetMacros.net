On a U.S. Supplement Facts panel, a proprietary blend can show the total weight without showing how much of each listed ingredient contributes to that total. An earlier ingredient weighs at least as much as one listed later. That tells you the order, but it leaves many possible formulas.

Check the label line that actually declares the amount

Both formulas keep the same ingredient order, although C differs by 130 mg. Dividing the total by three does not reveal C’s amount. For nutrients with established Daily Values, applicable U.S. rules require separate individual listings rather than hiding their quantities in this type of blend.

If an ad cites a study testing a known amount of an ingredient, the blend’s total cannot establish that the product contains that amount of the tested ingredient. Look for a separately declared quantity and the same preparation. Another guide explains how to read the serving size at the top of the panel.
