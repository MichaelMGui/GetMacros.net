Which question are you answering?

When comparing powder composition, use a fixed powder weight. When logging what you ate, use the grams on your scale or the exact serving listed for your package. The formulas here differ in other nutrients. The sodium numbers above come from 45 mg per 28 g whey serving and 170 mg per 24 g soy serving.

A composition match cannot establish the same digestibility, response to exercise, or suitability for an allergy. Check the ingredient and allergen statements. If you make the powder into a shake, add the milk, yogurt or other ingredients separately in the food diary.
