In a six-month randomized study of chewing gum, the researchers reported higher maximum bite force in the training group during the study. They did not detect significant changes in either masseter muscle thickness or mandibular shape. Bite force describes what participants could do during a measurement. Jaw shape describes structure.

Ask for direct evidence of the promised outcome

A camera angle can change the outline in a photo, and a bite test can change without a corresponding change in bone shape. A facial-structure claim needs a study that measures structure directly with comparable measurement conditions and an appropriate control. The absence of a detected change in this trial is specific to its participants, methods and schedule.
