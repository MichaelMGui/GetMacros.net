Half the weight of an order represents about half its listed nutrients only if it has a similar mix of ingredients. A bowl with most of the rice left and very little protein topping is a different case. Use documented component portions where you can identify them.

Three ways to record leftovers from an example build-your-own bowl
What is leftEntry that best describes it
Roughly half of a well-mixed bowlEstimate a half serving of your saved whole order.
Most of the rice and little of the meatEstimate each visible component separately, using its matching listed portion where possible.
Mixed sauce and small scraps with no clear amountsLog the parts you can estimate and mark the rest as uncertain.

Save the recipe or order with its original components

A market-specific ingredient calculator such as Chipotle’s U.S. tool lets you identify individual components. If you ate most of the chicken earlier and almost all the rice later, the separate entries make the two meals easier to interpret. When component data are missing, one approximate whole-order share can still be useful if you label the assumption clearly.
