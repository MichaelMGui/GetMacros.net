The table does not follow one bag of lentils through every step, so the dry and cooked entries cannot tell you precisely what boiling did to your batch.

Scale the food you actually weighed

A 150 g portion of the cooked green and brown reference scales to 228 µg, while 150 g of the cooked red reference scales to 54 µg. Both results describe those reference entries. To measure actual retention in a recipe, you would need a matched starting and finished batch, including their different weights and the fate of the cooking liquid.

The canned soup row has no usable folate number. The separate homemade soup entry has 6 µg per 100 g, but substituting that figure would assign a different recipe’s value to the tin in front of you.
