In the USDA records compared here, 100 g of a raw orange has 53.2 mg vitamin C. The same weight of pink or red grapefruit has 31.2 mg. A cup comparison narrows the difference because the grapefruit entry’s cup weighs 50 g more.

Use the portion you ate

The grapefruit cup includes juice in the stated measure. Neither cup is a universal fruit weight. For cut fruit, weigh the halves or sections you ate and use the matching raw entry. Different varieties and storage conditions may give different amounts.

Nutrition figures do not address grapefruit interactions. The FDA notes that prescription and over-the-counter medicine warnings may apply to the whole fruit as well as juice. Follow the instructions for your medicine and ask a pharmacist if the label is unclear. For other U.S. reference fruit amounts, see the Nutrient Density Explorer.
