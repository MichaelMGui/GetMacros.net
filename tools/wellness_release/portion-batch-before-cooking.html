If you weighed the dry oats into separate bowls, log the dry oats you used in each one. Added water changes the cooked weight. A bowl with more water can weigh more even if it started with the same amount of oats.

The oats use USDA record 173904 at 379 kcal per 100 g dry. This example assumes you ate all the oats and added only water. Milk, fruit or other ingredients need their own entries. USDA yield figures answer a shopping question about how much cooked food a dry amount typically makes. They do not override the ingredient weight you actually measured.

Use a different method if you cooked a single pot

When dividing a well-mixed cooked pot, you can use the fraction of the finished pot you ate. That requires the finished batch weight or an equal number of portions. In the two-bowl example, the dry split was already known. Save that basis in the food diary before topping the oats.
