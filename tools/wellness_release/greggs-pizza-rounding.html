Greggs’ UK guide lists 21 g protein for one pepperoni pizza portion and 41 g for its two-pack. Doubling the rounded single-portion figure gives 42 g. The published calories do double exactly at the shown precision. When you have the complete two-pack, its own row is the better match.

Could rounding produce these readings?

If a single portion rounds to 21 g of protein to the nearest gram, a value like 20.6 g could underlie it. Doubling that value gives 41.2 g, which would round to 41 g. Similarly, a hypothetical 1.43 g salt can round to 1.4 g while twice 1.43 g rounds to 2.9 g. These are numerical examples, not claims about Greggs’ unpublished recipe.

Use the source row for the amount and product you ate. Investigate a much larger discrepancy by checking the size, ingredients, source date and whether it is another variant. For related label math, see the Greggs salt to sodium guide.
