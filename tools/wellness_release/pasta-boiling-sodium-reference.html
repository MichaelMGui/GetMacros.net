For 150 g of cooked enriched pasta, the USDA’s cooked-with-salt entry scales to about 197 mg sodium. The no-added-salt entry scales to about 2 mg. Both list 5.8 g protein per 100 g. These two reference preparations tell you why the word “cooked” is only part of the food description.

Keep drained cooking water and sauce separate

The amount of salt that went into the pot is not necessarily the amount you ate. Some leaves with the water. These reference figures cannot estimate absorption in every kitchen because the recipe and draining differ. Log the closer match for plain pasta, and add any salted sauce, cheese or additional salt separately.
