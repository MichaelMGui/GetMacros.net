A 90 g half-cup of cooked spinach contains about 2.2 g fiber. A 172 g cup of boiled black beans has about 15.0 g. Spinach moves ahead when you compare 100 calories of each, because 100 calories of spinach is about 435 g of food.

To estimate fiber in dinner, start with the weight you plan to serve. The spinach serving here is half of the source’s 180 g cup and the fiber scales by one half. For an energy-density comparison, divide fiber per 100 g by energy per 100 g and multiply by 100. The tomato narrowly passes black beans on that second basis. Per 100 g, black beans remain at 8.7 g while tomato is 1.2 g.
