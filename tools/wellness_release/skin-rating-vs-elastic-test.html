A visible skin-aging grade compares an observed appearance against a scale or reference images. An elasticity measurement tests how skin deforms or recovers under defined instrument conditions. An improvement in one measurement does not automatically predict the result of the other.

What two kinds of skin measurements capture
MeasurementWhat the reader should check
Photonumeric ratingThe feature rated, reference photographs, training of assessors and whether assessors agreed.
Device elasticityThe model, tested site, settings and which derived response parameter was reported.

Match evidence to the specific promise

A consumer claim about how a line looks needs a relevant appearance outcome. If its only data come from elasticity at one location, check whether an appearance measure was recorded separately. The validation paper tests the usefulness of a defined visual scale. The human trial is an example of instrument measurements that describe different skin properties.
