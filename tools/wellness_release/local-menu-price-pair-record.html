A location-free screenshot leaves out a detail that can change the comparison.

Minimum records for a matched two-item price comparison
FieldWhat to capture
Location and timingStore, currency, date and when the two prices were observed.
Channel and scopePickup, delivery or in-store, and whether each line is one item or a combo.
Amount paidDisplayed item price and a consistent treatment of taxes, fees, tips and applied discounts.
Nutrition portionThe item, size and components corresponding to each price.

Use different channels only when they answer the question

Comparing pickup with delivery can be useful if that is the decision you’re making. Label it as a comparison between channels and make the treatment of order-wide costs explicit. For a same-store menu ranking, keep the channel fixed unless you can show how the difference was handled.
