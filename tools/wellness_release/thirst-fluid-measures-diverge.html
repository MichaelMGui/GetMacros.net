Thirst can subside before all exercise-related fluid loss has been replaced. In one exercise study, when people voluntarily stopped drinking, thirst and plasma osmolality had returned toward baseline, while the urine indicators and net fluid balance had not all returned to their starting state. Those measures describe different things and can move at different speeds.
