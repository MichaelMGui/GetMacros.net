What a swap does to a recipe

The yogurt figures are 65 × 110 ÷ 170 for sodium and 16 × 110 ÷ 170 for protein. If a dip uses 110 g of either dairy ingredient, the swap from these particular curds to the yogurt changes the subtotal by about −338 mg sodium and −3.6 g protein. If the dip makes four equal servings, the difference is about −85 mg sodium and −0.9 g protein per serving. Keep salt, seasoning and other ingredients separate.

Cottage cheese is often salted, but the amount on one tub cannot represent the category. If you prefer to keep the curds, compare plain products using grams first and the sodium row second. Use the food diary to save a measured recipe.
