A photo and an ingredients list can help identify a restaurant order. They cannot establish its calories by themselves. A named recipe, exact serving and quantity of any unpictured sauce are all needed for a documented estimate.

Find the menu number that belongs to the configuration

Federal menu requirements cover standard items at certain U.S. chains. An official calculator or written menu can be much closer to the photographed order than a visual estimate. Compare the item name, country and serving rather than a similar image. Even with the right item, a published standard is a reference for the served order.

If you cannot identify an addition, keep it unknown. For a simple example of checking the components of an actual menu listing, compare four nuggets and the complete Happy Meal.
