Greggs’ September 2026 UK nutrition guide gives 1.5 g salt for one standard sausage roll. Under the UK conversion convention, divide by 2.5 to get about 0.60 g of sodium, or 600 mg. The guide lists 6.2 g salt for the four-pack, so use that row for the whole pack.

Convert the unit after choosing the portion

Use grams of salt ÷ 2.5 × 1,000 to get milligrams of sodium. The third row divides the printed pack total by four and assumes equal shares. It is a calculation for that pack. Multiplying the separately rounded single-roll figure by four would give 6.0 g salt and 2,400 mg sodium. That does not give a reason to replace the published pack figure.

Track one number in the right field

If your diary field is in milligrams of sodium, use the converted number in that field once. If it is in grams of salt, keep the original figure. A salt-equivalent declaration can include naturally occurring sodium and sodium from other ingredients. It does not tell you how much table salt was physically added at the bakery. Use the food diary with the serving you ate, and check the restaurant guide for a different recipe or pack.
