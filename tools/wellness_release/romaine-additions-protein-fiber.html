Using USDA references, 100 g plain romaine has about 1.2 g protein and 2.1 g fiber. Add a typical weighed portion of roasted chicken, cooked chickpeas or feta, and the results move in different directions. Keeping the romaine constant makes the contribution of each topping visible.

Use your own amounts when you combine toppings

For example, adding both the chickpeas and feta in the table gives about 12.6 g protein before dressing. Start with the base once, add each topping contribution and round the final result. Put dressing, croutons and other ingredients on their own lines when you include them.

These are household examples using named reference foods, so brands and preparation can change the totals. Compare dishes and their components in the studio.
