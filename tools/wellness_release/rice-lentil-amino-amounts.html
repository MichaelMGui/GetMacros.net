The useful question for a rice and lentil bowl is what amounts of protein and each indispensable amino acid the ingredients contribute. “Complete” on its own tells you little about the serving. Both of the USDA entries used here report lysine and methionine, but at different amounts.

Two listed amino acids are a starting point

A full protein-quality calculation needs the other indispensable amino acids and a defined reference pattern. It also needs appropriate digestibility measurements when calculating a digestible amino-acid score. Adding the two reported ingredients does not supply those missing measurements.

For logging a homemade bowl, record the amount of each cooked ingredient. A different rice-to-lentil ratio produces different values. See how a separate protein-quality score is calculated.
