Researchers measuring a circadian shift often compare the timing of a biological marker before and after an intervention. Sleep onset latency is the time from trying to sleep to actually falling asleep. One can change without showing that the other changed.

Two different clocks in melatonin research
MeasureStarting pointWhat the result describes
Dim-light melatonin onsetA series of samples taken under controlled lightWhen a rise in the body’s own melatonin was detected.
Sleep onset latencyThe beginning of an attempt or opportunity to sleepHow long it took to fall asleep on the tested occasion.

Read the control group’s movement as well

In one small study the researchers moved the sleep schedule earlier in both conditions. The measured onset advanced about 1.3 hours with melatonin and about 0.7 hours with placebo. The difference in the group averages was about 36 minutes of shift in the biological marker. Those figures do not say anyone fell asleep 36 minutes faster.
