Dry-roasted sunflower seeds, almonds, hazelnuts and peanut butter supply vitamin E without using cooking oil. One ounce of sunflower seeds lists 7.4 mg of alpha-tocopherol in the NIH food table. A half-ounce topping would give about 3.7 mg, less than the 6.8 mg in a full ounce of almonds.

Use edible weight for the topping

In an example with a half-ounce of each of the reference seeds and almonds, the vitamin E subtotal is about 7.1 mg. The half-ounce of seeds gives 3.7 mg and half an ounce of almonds gives 3.4 mg. If you start with seeds in shells, use the edible portion to find a matching value. A spoonful can vary in weight.
