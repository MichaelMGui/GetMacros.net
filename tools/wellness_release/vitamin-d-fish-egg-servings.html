The NIH lists 16.2 mcg vitamin D in three ounces of cooked farmed rainbow trout and 14.2 mcg in three ounces of cooked sockeye salmon. A large scrambled egg is listed at 1.1 mcg. These are specific reference preparations rather than one value for all fish or eggs.

Use a different row for a different food

For 1½ oz of the same listed trout, the reference calculation is 8.1 mcg. If your meal uses another species or a canned product, choose that preparation instead. NIH also notes that fortified foods contribute vitamin D. Use the carton’s declared amount when comparing a specific fortified product.

Older sources may list international units beside micrograms. Those are two units for the same quantity. See the IU and microgram guide for the conversion.
