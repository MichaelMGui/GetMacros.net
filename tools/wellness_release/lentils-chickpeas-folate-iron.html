Half a cup of boiled lentils provides about 3.3 mg of iron and 179 micrograms of folate in USDA reference data. A half-cup of boiled chickpeas provides about 2.4 mg of iron and 141 micrograms of folate. Part of the difference comes from the lentil cup weighing more.

Use weighed shares when mixing both

A 100 g portion of 40 g lentils and 60 g chickpeas gives about 3.07 mg iron and 176 micrograms folate. Calculate 40% of the lentil row and 60% of the chickpea row on the same 100 g basis. There is no need to estimate what fraction of a cup the mix occupies.

These figures describe the amounts in food. They do not tell you how much iron a person absorbs. Use matched records for canned or salted versions if that is what you have. The explorer can show how iron and folate change between portions.
