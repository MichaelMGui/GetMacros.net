Turn the shared calorie figure back into grams. In these USDA reference entries, 400 kcal means about 242 g of cooked chicken breast or 303 g of boiled black beans. The same energy from plain romaine alone would take about 2.35 kg. All three figures answer the same mathematical question, but the implied meal portions are very different.

Use the basis that matches the decision

Comparing fixed energy is useful for seeing nutrient density. When planning a meal, inspect the implied weight first, then use the amount you would serve for the actual calculation. A plain salad base is not the same record as a finished salad with dressing and toppings.
