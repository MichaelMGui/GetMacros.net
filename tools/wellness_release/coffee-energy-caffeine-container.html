Calculate what you drank from the correct container

Half of the 12 fl oz can works out to 57 mg. Half of the 20 fl oz can works out to 99 mg. The calculation uses each can’s declared total. Avoid treating a “cup” in a study or a coffee machine as a fixed 12 fl oz. Brew recipe, beans and coffee size can all change the total.

If the coffee vendor does not state caffeine for the exact brew and cup, a range is more honest than a single exact value. For a separate question about how timing affects a research result, see the caffeine-and-sleep studies.
