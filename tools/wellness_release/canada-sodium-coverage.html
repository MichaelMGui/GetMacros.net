A calorie figure may be the only fact available

For Pizza Nova, the retained capture here lists calories for named in-store slices without sodium. These rows are useful for a calorie comparison within the listed portions. They cannot support sodium comparisons against another chain or a differently defined slice. The table does not describe what another Pizza Nova publication might supply.

Use three states in a comparison

Imagine side A lists 210 mg, side B lists 0 mg and side C gives only calories. For the known sodium values, side B comes before A. Side C belongs on a separate unknown line. If an order combines A and C, 210 mg is a subtotal for its known part. To compare the order against a sodium limit, you would still need side C’s figure.

We counted all retained Canadian rows, with each named variant, side and component kept separate. Numeric zero is kept within the known column and records what the retained source published, including its rounding limits. A chain with many rows contributes more to this count, so the table does not measure the proportion of Canadian restaurants or meals with complete sodium reporting.
