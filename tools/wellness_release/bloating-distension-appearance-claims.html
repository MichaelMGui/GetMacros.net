Bloating describes a feeling of fullness or swelling. Distension refers to the abdomen becoming larger. NIDDK notes that someone can feel bloated without measurable enlargement. A food list that promises to “debloat” should say whether it means a symptom, a measurement or a visual impression.

Three meanings hidden in one word
MeaningWhat a study would record
Feeling bloatedA reported sensation under defined conditions and at specified times.
DistensionA change in abdominal size using a repeatable measurement.
Looking different in a photoAn image under documented and comparable camera and posture conditions.

Keep digestive symptoms separate from appearance rankings

A photographed outline may vary with posture or capture conditions, and a symptom may change while the outline stays the same. If a source changes the outcome halfway through its argument, its evidence and conclusion no longer match. The examples above do not identify the cause of someone’s symptoms.
