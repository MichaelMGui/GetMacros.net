Water and air transfer heat differently. Immersion also covers a specific part of the body. In one crossover trial, the same 20 healthy adults completed a hot-water, traditional-sauna and far-infrared condition on separate days. A steam room was outside that comparison.

The researchers reported the largest acute thermoregulatory, cardiovascular and immune responses in the hot-water arm of this specific comparison. A larger initial response does not by itself establish a lasting benefit. For a claim about humid air, check for a steam intervention and the same measured endpoint.

One way to read a headline is to replace “heat therapy” with the exact intervention named in the paper. That preserves the difference between the test and a general claim about heat. The steam versus dry sauna comparison is a separate experiment with a very different protocol.
