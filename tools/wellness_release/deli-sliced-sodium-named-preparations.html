Its breast from a cooked, original-seasoning rotisserie chicken gives about 188 mg at that weight. The generic plain roasted breast entry gives about 44 mg. All three are separate U.S. reference foods.

The phrase can describe a deli seasoning or a cooking method. It does not supply a sodium number by itself. Ingredients and preparation can vary, especially with brine or added salt. A product’s own nutrition label is a better match if you have one.
