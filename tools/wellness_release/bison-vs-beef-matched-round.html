These two USDA top-round entries are close on energy at equal cooked weights. Broiled lean bison top round has 174 kcal per 100 g, while broiled lean select beef top round has 177 kcal. The pan-broiled ground bison entry is higher than both at 238 kcal. Comparing species without naming the cut can hide that difference.
