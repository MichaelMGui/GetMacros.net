Read the name, form and serving size together

When comparing an actual package, match the strength and the format. Another product from the same brand can use a different serving count. The equivalent unit in parentheses expresses the same amount. Keep only one unit when totaling like amounts.

Equal quantities per piece show a label comparison. They do not determine equivalent ingredients, individual absorption or a suitable intake. Follow the Supplement Facts reading guide for packages that contain more than one active ingredient.
