Look for both the assigned caffeine dose and the rules for any other caffeine. A study that removes a person’s usual coffee before giving a trial pill creates a different comparison from one that lets the person keep their ordinary intake. Bedtime and how the researchers measured sleep also belong beside the headline.

Four details that make a sleep experiment interpretable
DetailWhat to look for
Usual intakeWhether participants were regular users and what they could eat or drink outside assigned doses.
Dose and clockThe controlled amount and hours before the participant’s scheduled bedtime.
Matched comparisonWhether placebo used the same timing and kept other routine changes comparable.
Sleep measurementWhether the researchers logged perceived sleep, a device-based outcome, or both.

Watch for timing stated without an amount

The Drake study tested a 400 mg dose at different times relative to bedtime. A later crossover trial varied both amount and timing. Before comparing the results, check that the reported dose, timing and sleep outcome refer to the same condition. A cutoff reported for one amount should not be treated as a universal cutoff for every drink. Explore other ways to check evidence behind routine changes.
