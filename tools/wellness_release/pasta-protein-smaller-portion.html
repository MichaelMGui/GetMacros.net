A pasta with more protein per gram can still provide less protein if you serve a much smaller amount. Barilla’s U.S. red lentil penne page lists 25 g protein per 3.5 oz, about 99 g dry. The USDA generic enriched dry pasta entry has 13.04 g per 100 g. To compare dinner plates, weigh or estimate their dry shares before cooking.
