What counts as the same dish?

For this small audit, remove the terminal “ · Exclusive to Gatwick” location label for the grouping check only. Then require a match on chain, remaining name, serving text, source, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber and sodium, including which fields are absent. Sort by protein descending, then calories ascending, then name. The half-chicken entries at 579 kcal and 78.7 g protein occupy the first two raw slots. The airport Chicken Benedict at 619 kcal and 50 g protein stays distinct and appears in tenth position after grouping.

A location tag can matter when deciding where to order. Keep it on the row and use groups only when asking how many distinct choices the ranking contains.
