One 92 g drained tin of the USDA Atlantic sardines entry has about 22.7 g protein. An 85 g serving of the cooked Atlantic salmon entry has about 18.8 g, while its 178 g half-fillet portion has about 39.3 g. The can wins against the small serving in this dataset. The larger salmon portion supplies more.

Divide the drained tin’s unrounded protein estimate by 0.221 g of protein per gram of cooked salmon. The result is about 102.5 g. The calculation compares protein, while bones, retained oil, and other nutrients differ. It also uses the weight drained from the can, which can be smaller than the unopened package’s stated net amount.
