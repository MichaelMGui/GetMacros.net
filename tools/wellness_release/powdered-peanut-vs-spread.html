Match protein instead of full spoonfuls

Using the label, 13 × 7 ÷ 6 gives about 15 g dry powder to match 7 g protein. That is about 70 calories from the powder. A jar’s rounded spoonful values make this an estimate. If you mix the powder with plain water, the prepared spread gains weight without adding protein. Milk or other ingredients have their own entries.

This is a comparison of two grocery items, so leave texture and recipe performance out of the macro claim. In a smoothie, weigh the powder dry and log the liquid separately in the food diary.
