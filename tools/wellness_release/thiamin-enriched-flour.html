In USDA reference data, 100 g of enriched unbleached all-purpose flour has 0.785 mg of thiamin. The unenriched all-purpose reference has 0.120 mg at the same weight. A recipe made with 250 g of flour starts with about 2.0 mg from the enriched flour or 0.30 mg from the unenriched flour.

Read the ingredient before estimating the finished roll

The U.S. standard for enriched flour specifies thiamin and other nutrients. Check the actual flour and package since enrichment depends on the product. If you bake eight equal rolls, multiply the amount per 100 g by 2.5 and divide by eight to estimate the flour’s starting share.

The rolled and baked food may retain a different amount because heating can reduce thiamin. Eggs or milk can contribute separately.
