In two USDA entries, cooked tempeh has 19.9 g protein per 100 g and raw firm calcium-set tofu has 17.3 g. To estimate an amount that gives 20 g protein, that works out to about 101 g cooked tempeh or 116 g of that tofu before any further cooking.

Divide 20 by the grams of protein per 100 g, then multiply by 100. The calculation compares named portions, not the effects of fermentation or pressing alone. Both water and preparation can differ across products. If you weigh a baked piece of tofu, its cooked weight will differ from its original raw weight, so the label needs the original amount or a confirmed cooking yield.
