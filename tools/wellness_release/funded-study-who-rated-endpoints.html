Read the disclosure for specific roles rather than stopping at a company’s name. The cited collagen-supplement paper discloses that an employee worked for the company that prepared and supplied the active product and placebo. That explains a product role and a declared relationship. It does not tell you by itself who measured each outcome or chose the analysis.

Product and placebo supplyWho supplied the products, and how similar were they to participants?
Outcome measurementWhich results used a device, an investigator rating or an answer from a participant, and who knew the assignments?
Analysis and reportingWho analyzed the data, what was planned and which results were reported?

Evaluate the exact result being advertised

A disclosure can tell you where to look for safeguards. The result still depends on the comparison, the measurement method and the uncertainty. When a summary quotes only one outcome, look for the full set of outcomes and how the paper handled them. See how to trace a result back to its planned endpoint.
