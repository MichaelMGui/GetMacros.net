In the USDA pan-fried beef liver reference, 100 g contains 7,728 µg retinol. The amount therefore moves quickly when a portion gets larger. The U.S. adult tolerable upper intake level is 3,000 µg per day for preformed vitamin A from foods and supplements. It describes a usual-intake boundary used in risk assessment.

The table scales one reference food. NIH’s separate 3 oz example is close, with a small difference from the weight and use of total vitamin A activity. The upper limit specifically concerns preformed vitamin A, so you cannot assume the same limit applies to a plant food’s beta-carotene. Habitual amounts and other sources, including supplements, matter to an exposure assessment.
