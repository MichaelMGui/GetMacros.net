One saliva sample captures cortisol at a single time under specific conditions. It cannot show that day’s pattern or establish how stressed someone feels. Wake time matters, especially when a report describes a “morning” result without saying how soon after waking the sample was collected.

Ask what the report was designed to measure

Under a study of the awakening response, those are different points in the experiment even though the clock shows the same time. In daily-life research, electronic monitoring has shown why delays and inaccurate sample timing matter to the observed profile. The expert consensus also describes collection at multiple points and careful timing around waking.

A lab test ordered for a specific clinical question has its own purpose and instructions. A wellness score marketed as a general stress reading needs evidence that it measures the outcome it claims. See two different designs for measuring morning cortisol.
