A 150 g cup of FAGE Total 2% plain lists 15 g protein and 5 g total sugar. The same size FAGE Total 2% Strawberry split cup lists 12 g protein and 11 g total sugar, including 7 g added sugar. The second number describes the yogurt and strawberry preparation together.

Use the label for the way you ate it

If you mix both compartments and divide the mixture in two, an even half is about 60 calories and 6 g protein. Eating only the yogurt and leaving the fruit chamber behind is a different portion. The declared total does not break out that compartment’s calories or protein. Subtracting 7 g added sugar would also leave other ingredients and unknown weights in the calculation.

If you want to know the amounts for plain yogurt plus a measured amount of berries, use the exact plain yogurt entry and log the fruit as a separate ingredient in the food diary.
