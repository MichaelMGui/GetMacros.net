USDA’s dried, sulfured apricot reference has 7.3 g fiber and 1 mg vitamin C per 100 g. The fresh apricot reference has 2 g fiber and 10 mg vitamin C per 100 g. Removing water can concentrate some values by weight. A different nutrient can still come out lower in the reference comparison.

Use a measured handful

The last two rows use the source’s weights of 35 g for one fresh apricot and 3.5 g for a dried half. Real pieces vary. Weigh the serving if precision matters. These are two snack portions, with no assumption that the dried halves came from those fresh fruits.

The reference entries also cannot isolate drying from other differences such as variety, storage or processing. Keep the package ingredient list and exact form beside the serving. See the nutrition label guide for how to log the actual amount.
