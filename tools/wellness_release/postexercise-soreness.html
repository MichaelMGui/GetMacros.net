Soreness can be one sign of a response to unfamiliar exercise. It is a separate measure from the force you can produce, range of motion or a blood marker. A number on a soreness scale cannot be converted into a percentage of strength recovered.

Researchers used the greatest decline in maximal voluntary strength to define the groups, then compared soreness and other markers. Soreness and the other measures followed the group pattern. That does not make the group centers soreness cutoffs. They are strength measures and do not give the strength of an individual from a soreness rating alone.
