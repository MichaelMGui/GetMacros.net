A trial that compares a collagen powder with maltodextrin tests the difference between those two powders under its conditions. In one eight-week trial, 57 adults received 10 g of hydrolyzed collagen and 55 received 10 g of maltodextrin. Equal powder weight does not equate the type or amount of protein.
