“Adult” means whatever age cutoff a study actually uses. In one German claims analysis, the reported group over 25 had a 1.50% recorded prevalence in 2020. The group aged 25 and under had 3.88%. These are diagnosis claims in defined age groups, which give a different signal from examining people for facial acne.

Before comparing two percentages, copy the age limits, the rule for counting a case, and who could enter each denominator. In a food study, also check whether the sample included people already being treated. An age breakdown can describe a sample without showing that a proposed dietary change works in that group.
