GetMacros can restore a valid diary snapshot that contains calories and leaves other nutrients unknown. It cannot import a plain list of dates and calories as a GetMacros backup. A valid backup includes the food identity, portion basis and saved nutrition details needed to check each entry.

Energy alone does not give you the macros

Two meals can have the same calories and different amounts of protein, carbohydrates, fat and fiber. If you see a partial subtotal after restoring a calories-only entry alongside a complete entry, the subtotal only adds the known values. It cannot establish the full day’s nutrient intake.

Keep the original file and test imports with a copy. If you want to record a new food from a paper label, add the exact portion in the diary using the figures the label actually reports. Follow the backup and restore workflow for a valid GetMacros export.
