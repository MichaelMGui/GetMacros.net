One USDA cup of prepared edamame contains about 18.4 g protein. A USDA cup of mature soybeans, boiled without salt, contains about 31.3 g. The mature beans have more protein per 100 g and the reference cup weighs 17 g more, so both factors contribute to the gap.

Separate what the cup weighs from what the beans contain

At the same 155 g weight, the mature beans would supply about 28.2 g, about 9.8 g more than the edamame. The remaining 17 g in the mature bean cup adds about 3.1 g more protein. The values describe these particular USDA preparations. They cannot isolate what ripeness alone does, since water and preparation differ.

Use the weight of the beans you eat and exclude discarded pods. If you use a salted or seasoned packet, its own label is the better match for that product. For a soup or grain bowl, compare the same measured scoop before adding other ingredients in the food diary.
