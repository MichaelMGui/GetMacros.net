In a small lab study after one short night, alertness and performance recovered earlier after a 10-minute afternoon nap than after a 30-minute nap. The researchers watched the first hour after waking. The result cannot rank the naps for an entire day or tell you what happens that night.

What happened during the hour after the nap in one experiment
Condition after the restricted nightWhat the researchers reported
No napAlertness and performance did not improve over the testing period
10-minute napSome subjective alertness and task results improved soon after waking and through the tested hour
30-minute napSeveral results dipped immediately after waking and partially recovered by the final test

A study can end before a different effect sets in

Each condition followed a night with 4.7 hours of sleep. The twelve participants were tested before and at five, 35 and 60 minutes after the nap. The initial dip could relate to sleep inertia, the grogginess immediately after waking. It says little about any advantage later that afternoon.

A separate study of repeated restriction allowed a 45-minute nap opportunity. It observed less sleepiness after the nap but did not see that benefit the next morning.
