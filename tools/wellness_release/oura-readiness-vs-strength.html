Oura Readiness summarizes several signals, including recent and longer-term patterns. It does not measure the force a muscle can produce. A score can be useful as a personal trend as long as you read what contributed to it.

Three ways to talk about recovery
SignalWhat it comparesWhat a change means
Readiness heart-rate contributionLowest heart rate from the previous night against the person’s longer-term averageOne input shifted relative to its baseline.
Readiness HRV balanceA weighted recent HRV average against a longer-term referenceThe app summarizes changes across several days.
Knee-flexor force in a recovery studyMeasured static force at follow-up against the person’s previous performanceThat specific task recovered by a measured amount.

The running study cited here assessed soreness and force separately. A wearable score can move for reasons outside that one task. Check the underlying contributors and keep the feeling of soreness as its own observation. A new device or firmware can also affect what a score means.
