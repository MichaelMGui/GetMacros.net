At 100 g of cooked edible weight, the USDA’s roasted thigh meat-with-skin record has 232 calories. Its meat-only record has 179. Selenium and zinc stay much closer than the energy values for these two preparations.

Weigh the preparation you chose

For an 85 g portion of the meat-only entry, logging about 152 calories and 21.1 g protein follows the published basis. If you ate the skin, switch to the matching record and use the weight of the skin and meat together. Leaving bone and other refuse out keeps the weight aligned with both entries.

These are separate reference records, so their difference does not measure the exact nutrition of the skin removed from a particular thigh. Seasoning and other additions also need their own entries. See the bone-in purchase and edible yield example if you start with the weight from the store.
