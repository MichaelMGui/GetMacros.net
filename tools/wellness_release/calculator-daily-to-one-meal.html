The GetMacros calculator shows a daily estimate first. Its meal search waits for a separate limit. If the daily example is 2,100 kcal, selecting a quarter of the day fills in 525 kcal and selecting a third fills in 700 kcal. You can also enter an amount you choose.

Check whether the result represents your whole order

Use “Find matching meals” after entering or choosing the limit. The link sends the one-meal limit to the finder. It does not include body measurements or the full calculation. If you have no preference, there is also a browse link without a calorie limit.

A sandwich listing may describe the sandwich alone. If you use the limit for dinner, check the side, drink and other additions as well. Open the macro calculator.
