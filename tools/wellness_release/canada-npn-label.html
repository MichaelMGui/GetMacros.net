A Canadian Natural Product Number identifies a licensed natural health product. You can use the eight digits to find the specific licence in Health Canada’s database. A U.S. Supplement Facts panel states information about the labeled ingredients and amount per serving. It does not provide a Canadian licence check.

Start with the NPN instead of a shared brand name

On the Health Canada search page, select the NPN or DIN-HM field and enter the exact number. Then compare the result to the package and check the product’s listed conditions and risk information. Market notification is voluntary. The market flag is not proof that a store has the product on a particular day.

U.S. packaging can look familiar and still represent a different formulation or jurisdiction. Check the product in the database for the country where it is sold. The elemental magnesium example shows how to distinguish the declared mineral amount from the form.
