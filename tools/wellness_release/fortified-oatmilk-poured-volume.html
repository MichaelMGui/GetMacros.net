Oatly’s U.S. 32 fl oz Original Oatmilk listing shows 3.6 µg vitamin D per 240 mL serving and about four servings per carton. That gives roughly 14.4 µg for the carton from the displayed values. The U.S. 8 fl oz package lists 3.6 µg for the whole 236 mL package.

Match the package before multiplying

The small package is labeled as a single serving even though its stated volume is a little different from the large carton’s standard serving. For the 180 mL example, divide 180 by 240 before multiplying the amount per serving. The full-carton estimate uses the label’s approximate serving count and should not be treated as a precise product assay.

Other sizes, markets, and oat drink recipes may use different fortification. Use the declaration on the label you have whenever available. Compare nutrient amounts in other measured portions.
