If another session starts soon, what happens during the first hours of recovery can affect the conditions under which the next session begins. The Ivy experiment sampled recovery after exercise and compared carbohydrate given promptly with carbohydrate given after a delay. The running study also examined a subsequent exercise test. A measurement inside the muscle and an outcome during the next effort are distinct, even when both studies use carbohydrate during recovery.
