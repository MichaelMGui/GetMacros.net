When a headline says protein before sleep improved “recovery,” look for the specific protein pool or function that the researchers tracked. A change in how much protein the whole body retains over a single night can coexist with little measurable change in the rate of building one fraction of muscle protein.

Look at the comparison before drawing a conclusion about timing

These acute studies compared protein taken before sleep with a non-protein condition. They did not compare the same amount of protein moved to an earlier time across all groups. Their samples came from healthy young men, and the measurements took place overnight after specific bouts of exercise. The papers cannot establish that bedtime is better for everyone or that a change in one night's synthesis rate will translate to long-term strength.
