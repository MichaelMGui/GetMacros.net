Check head position before using a ruler

Compare the head angle and camera height across the two images. A photo can be cropped to the same scale while the head remains pitched differently. Do not use these averages as a correction factor for an individual photo. They describe one adjustment and a particular set of measured landmarks.

If a diet or chewing claim relies on a single pair of profile images, the photos need comparable positioning and separate evidence that the claimed intervention caused a change. See what camera distance adds to that comparison.
