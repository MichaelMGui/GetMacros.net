Elemental magnesium is the amount of magnesium supplied by the compound. On a U.S. Supplement Facts panel, a line reading “250 mg Magnesium (as Magnesium Oxide)” declares 250 mg of magnesium. The parenthetical name identifies the source form.

Read amount, form and serving size together

The two products declare the same elemental amount for a serving. Dividing by the number of units in each serving changes the per-unit comparison. A number on the front of the package is easiest to interpret once you have confirmed what the Supplement Facts panel calls a serving.

This calculation does not tell you the total compound weight or the amount a person absorbs. Those are separate questions. If you want to understand the latter claim, read what a magnesium form study actually measured.
