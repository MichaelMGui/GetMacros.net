Beef liver and steak can have similar energy in these USDA entries while differing greatly in retinol. For 75 g of cooked food, the pan-fried liver and broiled lean top-round steak give about 131 and 133 kcal respectively. A steak entry would still give a very different vitamin A result from a liver entry.

Keep the cut, preparation and edible portion together

This comparison uses a particular lean steak and a pan-fried organ entry. It does not isolate how cooking alone changes the numbers. If you log beef liver in a recipe, use its own cooked entry and retain the portion you ate. Cooking oil or accompanying ingredients may require separate entries depending on the source description.
