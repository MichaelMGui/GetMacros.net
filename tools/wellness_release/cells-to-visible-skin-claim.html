Research on cells can show that a measured signal changed under laboratory conditions. A study of dermal fibroblasts and a copper peptide examined secretion of a signaling factor. The result describes cells under the test conditions. A claim about how a finished product changes appearance requires a separate study.

What changes as the evidence moves from cells to people
Level of evidenceQuestion to ask
Cultured cellsWhat signal was measured, in which cells and compared with which control?
Finished productDoes the study actually test the formulation that is marketed?
Appearance in peopleWas a relevant visible outcome prespecified and measured against a suitable comparator?

Look for the missing link in the advertised claim

Human cells in a dish make the study human-derived, but they are not a group of people applying the product. A plausible mechanism may justify more research. It does not tell you the size, visibility or persistence of any effect in everyday use.

Check whether a cited reference reports a completed human trial.
