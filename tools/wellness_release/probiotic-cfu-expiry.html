Start with the words beside the number. A probiotic label that gives colony-forming units (CFU) through expiry is making a different claim from a label that gives the count when manufactured. Some microorganisms can lose viability during storage. Weight in milligrams alone cannot give a live count.

Match the timing, then the strain

First align whether the amount is per capsule, per sachet, or per full serving. Then match the point in shelf life. The two fictional numbers cannot tell you which container has more viable organisms at the time you open it.

Evidence for a particular purpose still depends on the organism’s strain, the studied formulation and the people in the trial. A blend total may also leave each strain’s amount undisclosed. Use the Claim Evidence Library to check how a label claim connects to the study behind it.
