Two large hard-boiled eggs add up to 100 g of edible food in the USDA reference entry. That portion provides 12.6 g protein, 2.2 micrograms vitamin D, 1.11 micrograms B12 and about 31 micrograms selenium. The shell is excluded.

What a measured side changes

Adding 90 g of cooked unsalted spinach from reference 168463 contributes about 131 micrograms of food folate and 1.35 micrograms selenium. The combined reference estimates are about 175 micrograms folate and 32.2 micrograms selenium. In this pair, the spinach accounts for most of the folate, and the eggs account for most of the selenium.

If your eggs are a different size, weigh them peeled and scale the reference. Use a prepared entry or separate ingredients for fried or scrambled eggs with added fat or dairy. Compare more portions in the nutrient explorer.
