In social research, hypergamy describes a relationship where one partner is higher on a named measure, such as completed education. The definition needs both a measure and a direction. Online posts sometimes use the word to imply a general ranking of people. That broader claim does not follow from a study about educational categories.

Look up who was counted

A Greek census study compared educational pairings across cohorts. A separate cross-country analysis examined how educational pairings related to the distribution of education and other structural factors. Those are studies of recorded couples. Their denominators depend on the sample and the category definitions. The data do not provide a universal account of online dating or personal preferences.

Translate the online claim

Before repeating a claim about how common hypergamy is, write down the exact variable, whose direction is being measured, the denominator and the year. If the caption switches from education to appearance or income, it has changed the question.
