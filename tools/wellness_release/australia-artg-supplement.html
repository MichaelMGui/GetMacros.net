For an Australian complementary medicine with an AUST number, enter the digits into the Therapeutic Goods Administration’s register and compare the exact product, sponsor and ingredients. The prefix tells you which inclusion pathway applies. Some nutrition products are regulated as foods and are outside this medicine lookup.

Why the number matters more than a familiar name

The register has a Blackmores Vitamin C 500 entry at 318912 listing ascorbic acid. It also has a similarly named Blackmores Vitamin C 500MG entry at 136142. Both show Blackmores Ltd as sponsor. A search by name can therefore bring up a plausible-looking result with different digits. Use the number from the label and then read the associated public summary.

The entry helps identify the local product and its status. It cannot tell you whether an online seller has genuine stock or whether a product is suitable for you. For a different kind of check, see the guide to matching an exact sports supplement batch.
