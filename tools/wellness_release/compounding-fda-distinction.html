In the U.S., compounding and FDA approval are separate pathways. Compounding can meet particular patient needs, such as a different dosage form. The FDA explains that it does not verify the safety, effectiveness or manufacturing quality of a compounded drug through the same premarket approval process.

Find the subject of the approval claim

An advertisement might say that a preparation uses the same ingredient as an approved medicine. The approval applies to the finished product reviewed by the FDA. Changing how an ingredient is formulated or manufactured can change the resulting preparation. An approved generic has its own application and regulatory requirements. The FDA discusses it separately from compounding.
