Paul Saladino’s current guide page uses “Animal-Based” for a framework that includes animal foods, fruit, honey and raw dairy. The same page lists a separate “Carnivore Elimination Reset” with a more restrictive starting set. Those are his named frameworks. The words alone are less precise when people use them in other contexts.

Check the claim against the food list

If a video calls his Animal-Based framework “zero carb,” that does not match the description on the guide page, which explicitly includes foods such as fruit and honey. If another post cites a survey of carnivore dieters to support the Animal-Based framework, check what the participants actually reported eating. The carnivore survey guide shows why that study cannot establish outcomes for a named menu from a different source.

Keep promotion separate from food safety

The creator’s inclusion of raw dairy establishes what his guide promotes. Independent food-safety guidance is needed to evaluate that recommendation.
