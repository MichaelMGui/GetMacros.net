When an entry already includes every ingredient you ate, logging those same ingredients separately adds them again. This can happen when you save a recipe with a topping and also keep the topping as a standalone entry.

A fictional one-bowl recipe and a half-bowl portion

Base recipe300 kcal150 kcal
Topping already in the saved recipe80 kcal40 kcal
Complete bowl380 kcal190 kcal

Check the name, portion and ingredients

Logging half the complete bowl and half the topping gives 230 kcal in this example. The meal contained 190 kcal, since the complete half already had the topping. You could log the complete half once or log the separate 150 kcal base and 40 kcal topping. An extra topping you added at the table would have its own entry.

If you see a duplicate, review the date, meal and amounts before removing it. For a regularly saved dish, make a clear name for the base recipe and any version that already includes toppings. Open your diary to check a saved portion.
