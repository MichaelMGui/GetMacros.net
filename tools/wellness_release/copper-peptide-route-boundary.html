A topical copper peptide study cannot establish the safety or efficacy of an injectable product. One original GHK-Cu-related experiment applied a copper tripeptide complex to wounds in irradiated rats. It used a specific animal model, route and outcome. Those conditions do not reproduce a cosmetic cream on intact human skin or a compounded injection.

Keep route in the evidence summary

A shared ingredient name does not demonstrate that a different product produces the same exposure in the body. When an advertisement cites the animal paper, check the model and route before reading the result. The U.S. FDA statement addresses a separate risk question and does not turn topical evidence into human injection data.
