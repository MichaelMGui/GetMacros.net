A sleep routine can aim for consistent timing, more time in bed, or some other measurable outcome. If a post calls all of those “sleepmaxxing,” choose the outcome first. A published study of students measured sleep regularity and related it to other outcomes. That does not make regularity interchangeable with total time asleep.

Report what changed

Neither example measures how much time the person actually slept. If you want to know how timing changes, record actual bedtime and wake time. If you want to know whether nights became longer, use an estimate of actual sleep duration. Adding several routine changes at once makes it harder to attribute any difference to one step.
