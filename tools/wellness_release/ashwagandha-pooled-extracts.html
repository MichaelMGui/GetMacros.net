A meta-analysis can combine results from several ashwagandha trials. It cannot rank two extracts just because each was compared with placebo in a different study. Participants, formulations, duration and outcome scales may all differ. The 2022 synthesis reported substantial variation across studies.

Read a pooled ashwagandha result at three levels
LevelWhat it can establish
One trialHow its stated extract compared with its own control in the eligible group.
Evidence synthesisWhat pattern the included comparisons show under the specified pooling method and how much they vary.
Direct comparison of extractsWhether a matched study assigned the different products and used the same outcome and time point.
