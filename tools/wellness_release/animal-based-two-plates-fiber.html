Use the actual meal rather than the name of the eating pattern

People can use broad diet names for meals with different ingredients. An estimated fiber total belongs to the recorded plate, not to everyone who follows the same label. If the side is smaller, scale just its contribution. A 50 g portion of the same beans would give about 4.4 g fiber.
