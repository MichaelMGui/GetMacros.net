Subway’s Chicken Breast salad page for the Republic of Ireland lists 102 calories and 17 g protein for its 197 g serving. The UK page for the same menu name lists 148 calories and 20 g protein for 219 g. The difference remains after adjusting for a common weight.

What the 22 g difference does and does not explain

If the Irish salad’s calorie density were scaled to 219 g, it would be about 113 kcal using the published values. The UK listing is still higher than that. The pages describe customization but do not isolate the component responsible. The table is for the listed salads, and it does not tell you the effect of adding your own sauce or cheese.
