For bone-in, skin-on thighs of about 4 oz each, USDA’s institutional buying guide estimates about 1.7 oz of cooked meat per piece if you discard the skin and bone. That is roughly 48 g of edible meat. Size, trimming and cooking can change the amount, so weigh the cooked meat if you already have it.

USDA SR Legacy record 172388 for roasted thigh meat lists 24.8 g protein per 100 g. The 48 g estimate would therefore contribute about 12 g protein. A portion of actual deboned meat that weighs 70 g would contribute about 17 g using the same reference.

Thighs sold with backs attached and already skinless thighs have their own yields. Weighing leftovers separately is another way to distinguish what you ate from what stayed on the plate. Log the meat in the food diary and account for sauce or added oil separately when relevant.
