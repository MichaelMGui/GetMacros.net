Look for a matched human comparison with a prespecified visible outcome, time point and method. A study can measure skin properties with several instruments, while a marketing headline picks only the smallest p-value. An informative report shows which comparison the researchers planned and what else they measured.

Audit the outcome a skin supplement advertisement quotes
FindWhat it helps establish
Outcome and timing in the protocolWhether the advertised measurement and time were identified before results were seen.
Between-group resultHow the change compares with placebo or the study’s stated comparator.
Units and uncertaintyThe size of the difference and how precisely the study estimated it.
Other outcomes and sitesWhether the advertised result was one of many opportunities to find a difference.

Ask if the measured effect would be visible

The cited collagen trial is an example of recording multiple skin properties. An instrument result may be useful without showing that observers would notice a change.
