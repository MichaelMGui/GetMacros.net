A heavier scale reading during a creatine study reports a change in total mass. In a 28-day placebo-controlled study of people who trained with weights, the group given creatine had increases in total body water and body mass. The study also measured creatine within muscle. That last measurement tracks the substance in sampled tissue, while a direct measure of tissue growth would require a different outcome.

Read the endpoint before interpreting the headline

The researchers did not detect a change in fluid distribution between the compartments they measured. Their result allows a group-level observation about water and weight under those study conditions. An at-home scale cannot break out someone’s precise water change, and the amount of creatine in a small biopsy does not quantify kilograms of newly built muscle.
