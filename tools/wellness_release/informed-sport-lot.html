To verify an Informed Sport claim, match the product and the lot printed on your package to the certification program’s searchable batch listing. The program screens batches of certified products for substances prohibited in sport. Certification applies to a particular product, and a company’s other products can have different coverage.

Read the lot code and what the program actually checks

Use the lot or batch identifier, often printed near the date. A barcode can take you to the product, but it usually identifies a retail product rather than one production lot. The program describes separate screening for flavour variants. If your search returns an apparently identical tin without your exact batch, the search has not confirmed that tin.

Sport Integrity Australia describes batch testing as a way to reduce doping risk. It cannot guarantee safety for an individual athlete or prove the claimed performance benefit. Compare the scope of a batch screen with the other supplement-label checks.
