Sleep duration measures the amount of sleep. Sleep regularity describes how consistently periods of sleep and wake repeat. Two schedules can both contain eight hours each night while starting and ending at very different times.

How the comparison works

The Sleep Regularity Index compares whether a person is in the same sleep or wake state at times one day apart across a record. In the table, midnight is one illustrative comparison. Most points in the two examples could still match. Researchers use many points across multiple days. Two bedtimes alone do not provide a study-grade index.

Read what a cohort can establish

Windred and colleagues calculated regularity from accelerometer data for 60,977 UK Biobank participants and examined later outcomes. The cohort can describe which patterns were associated with outcomes under its analyses. A study that randomly assigns schedules would answer a different causal question. It is also important to check whether a headline uses a regularity index, variation in bedtime or another measure. Those quantities are not interchangeable.
