Start with what the participants did. A trial of powdered tart cherry around one intense lower-body session tests recovery under one concentrated workload. The same total sets spread over a longer training period could represent a very different exercise pattern.

Workload details that belong beside a recovery claim
DetailWhy it matters
Exercise and loadA squat at a stated relative intensity differs from an endurance session or a low-load movement.
Repetitions and restTen sets of ten repetitions in one session gives 100 repetitions in that window. Dividing them across five sessions changes the stress and recovery timing.
Training experienceResults from resistance-trained participants may differ from people returning after a break or training a movement for the first time.

Use the workload to choose a relevant comparison

The tart cherry experiment used a defined strength bout. In contrast, the long training experiment assigned recovery approaches after repeated exercise and assessed adaptation over time. Even if both mention leg strength, they do not estimate the same effect. State the original workload and outcome whenever you summarize a result.
