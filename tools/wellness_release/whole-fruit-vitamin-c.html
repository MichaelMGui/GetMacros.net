One 69 g green kiwi provides about 64 mg of vitamin C in USDA reference data. A 131 g orange provides about 70 mg. The kiwi has more vitamin C per gram, but the USDA’s example orange is almost twice as heavy. One 81 g SunGold kiwi and one 157 g small papaya both provide more vitamin C than either in these specific portions.

Use a fruit’s edible weight when you can

For a green kiwi, 92.7 × 69 ÷ 100 gives roughly 64 mg. A 90 g edible portion of the same reference variety would work out to about 83 mg. Weighing the fruit after removing the parts you discard makes a closer estimate than assuming every piece weighs the same.

SunGold is a named variety in the USDA release. Do not apply its value to all kiwis. And when you want to compare juices, use a juice record with its own serving weight. See the nutrient explorer for more food comparisons.
