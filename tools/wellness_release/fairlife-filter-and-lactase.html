The U.S. fairlife 2% reduced-fat ultra-filtered milk label lists 13 g protein in a 240 mL cup. The manufacturer explains that its filtration changes the concentration of components already in milk. The ingredient list names ultra-filtered milk, lactase enzyme and vitamins A and D. It gives separate clues about how the milk is processed and what is added.

Read the claims as three separate questions

The filtration description explains the change in concentration. The ingredient list also shows lactase, an enzyme that breaks down lactose, and the vitamin ingredients identify fortification. The 6 g is the total sugar line, which can still contain sugars even when the product is labeled lactose-free.

If you pour 180 mL of the specific fairlife product, three-quarters of the manufacturer’s cup gives about 9.75 g protein and 4.5 g total sugar before label rounding. For another brand or country, scale that exact label. Compare fixed portions in GetMacros.
