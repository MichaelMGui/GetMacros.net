Recovery has to be defined by the outcome. A marathon study can track how sore runners feel alongside how a leg moves and how a blood marker changes. Return to normal on one measure does not establish recovery on the others, even for the same participant.

Check whether the last observation was really the endpoint

If a paper stops collecting on a particular day, an unresolved measure at that point cannot tell you when it returned to baseline. Early soreness measured near the finish also means something different from a later rating. The linked studies look at outcomes over time instead of using one finish-line snapshot.
