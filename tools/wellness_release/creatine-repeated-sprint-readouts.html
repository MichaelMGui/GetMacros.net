In repeated-sprint research, “more power” could mean the highest instant, an average across one effort, or a difference across several efforts. A creatine study that tests one maximal sprint and a sequence of short sprints can ask both kinds of questions. The answers do not automatically transfer to sustained endurance or a different sport.

How sprint outcomes describe different parts of the same trial
OutcomeQuestion
Peak powerWhat was the highest recorded output during the measured effort?
Average powerHow much power was produced on average throughout the defined effort?
Performance across repetitionsWhat happened as efforts were repeated under the specified recovery intervals?

Read the protocol before comparing results

The cycling paper includes both a single short maximal effort and a sequence of repeated efforts. A separate experiment used repeated treadmill sprints. Even when both report a power measurement, the movement, resistance, rest periods and time window give that number its meaning. Read the relevant table or figure instead of assuming a result for one repetition describes the whole sequence.
