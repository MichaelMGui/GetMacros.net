A #NoFilter tag is a claim about how an image was made. Even if the claim is accurate, it cannot show that a skincare product, supplement or food caused a visible change. Two images still need comparable conditions and a way to account for other changes.

An advert promising the same resultWhat independent evidence shows a comparable change under defined conditions?

A filter matters most when it changes exactly what the product claims

The UK Advertising Standards Authority reviewed an influencer promotion for tanning and moisturising products. Its reasoning focused on whether the effect of a beauty filter could be distinguished from an effect on the appearance of skin claimed for the product. A reader encountering a nutrition promotion can apply the same question to a focus on tone, texture or shine. Ask for a measure beyond a caption.

If a post provides only a short montage, it may show the creator’s experience without establishing what caused it. For a closer look at photo setup, use the before-and-after comparison guide.
