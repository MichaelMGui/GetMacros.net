The USDA reference for one cup of regular cooked long-grain white rice without added salt is 158 g. It works out to about 205 kcal. A scoop from your own pot can weigh more or less, so use a scale when you want the entry to follow what was actually served.

Convert the weight you actually measure

Set a bowl on the scale and tare it. Add the cooked rice, then multiply its weight in grams by 130 and divide by 100. The two examples differ by about 86 kcal despite using the exact same rice entry. They illustrate plausible weights, not a range measured for all household cups.

The 158 g reference applies to cooked rice. A cup of dry rice is a different amount and a different food record. If you cook with oil, stock or other grains, use a matching recipe in the food diary or add those ingredients separately.
