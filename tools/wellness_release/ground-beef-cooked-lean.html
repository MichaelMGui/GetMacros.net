In the USDA’s broiled entries, an 85 g patty from 80/20 ground beef contains about 230 kcal, and one from 90/10 ground beef contains about 184 kcal. Measured at the same cooked weight, the leaner patty saves about 45 kcal before toppings, with similar protein.

The raw blend name is not the cooked fat percentage

The blend names distinguish the meats before cooking. Cooked weights and fat contents shift as juices and fat are lost. To compute the broiled example, multiply the 100 g entries by 0.85. If you weigh two patties before broiling instead, save that starting weight and compare a labeled raw amount or use a documented yield.

Use the preparation corresponding to your meal. A pan-broiled patty or drained crumbles can have different values. Add the bun, toppings and cooking additions separately in the food diary.
