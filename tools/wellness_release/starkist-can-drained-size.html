The 5 oz net weight describes the packaged contents, including liquid. StarKist’s two chunk-light can examples each list one drained can as 113 g for Nutrition Facts. If you ate half the drained fish, use half the label’s serving amount.

Can weight and serving weight are separate

Five ounces converts to about 142 g. Scaling the 113 g nutrient record to 142 g would overstate a fully drained can by about 26% in this example. If the drained fish weighs differently on your scale, use that weight with the correct drained recipe and treat the result as an estimate.
