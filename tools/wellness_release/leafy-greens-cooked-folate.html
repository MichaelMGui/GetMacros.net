In the USDA’s boiled, drained, unsalted entries, half a cup of spinach provides about 131 micrograms of folate. A half-cup of turnip greens provides about 85 micrograms and collards about 15. The numbers compare specific cooked preparations.

Calculate a mixed side from weighed greens

If you serve 45 g of cooked spinach and 45 g of cooked turnip greens, the estimated subtotal is (146 × 0.45) + (118 × 0.45), or about 119 micrograms. Using those weights avoids assuming that both greens pack the same way into the cup.

These numbers are for naturally occurring food folate. If your recipe also contains enriched grain, check that ingredient for folic acid and the correct dietary folate equivalents. See the vitamins and minerals collection for the distinction between folate amounts and label units.
