Look at who took part before applying a hair supplement study to a celebrity ad. A marine-extract trial enrolled 96 women ages 21–55 with subclinical thinning or hair loss and included a placebo. A separate open-label study enrolled people with thinning in several demographic groups and used formulations tailored to those groups.

Follow the product evidence rather than the spokesperson

Different causes of hair changes can produce similar photos. A study of self-perceived thinning does not automatically tell you how a different diagnosed condition would respond. Check which outcome the study measured and whether a product used in a recent ad still has the tested formulation.
