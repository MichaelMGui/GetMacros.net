A stick is the first common unit to check. LMNT lists 1,000 mg sodium for a stick of its zero-sugar mix. The original U.S. Liquid I.V. Lemon Lime label lists 500 mg sodium and 11 g total sugar in its packet. Those are separate amounts, and the products’ other electrolytes are separate label lines.

Distinguish dilution from amount consumed

If you mix a packet into a large bottle and drink all of it, you still consume the listed amount for one packet. If you drink exactly half of a well-mixed bottle, estimate half the sodium and sugar. Adding more water alone changes concentration, not the amount originally placed in the bottle.
