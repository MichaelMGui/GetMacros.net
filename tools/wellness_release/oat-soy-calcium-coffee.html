Silk Unsweet Soymilk lists 470 mg calcium per 240 mL cup. Oatly’s U.S. chilled Unsweetened oat drink lists 320 mg per 240 mL. A 60 mL pour is one-quarter of both labels and gives about 118 mg or 80 mg, respectively.

Measure the amount you actually pour

Use the volume of the plant drink, not the volume of the whole coffee. For another carton, multiply its calcium amount by your pour in milliliters and divide by the milliliters in its listed serving. If it gives only a percentage, check the jurisdiction’s reference and the original package before converting.

The comparison is about these recipes and their labels. It does not put a single calcium number on soy or oats.
