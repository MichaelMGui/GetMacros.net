A Brazil nut has no single fixed amount of selenium. In an Amazon-region study, the median concentration measured in nuts from Mato Grosso was 2.07 milligrams per kilogram. The median measured for Amazonas was 68.15 mg per kilogram. Those are medians in the sampled nuts, not declarations for every bag from either region.

What changed even with the same mass?

One milligram per kilogram is one microgram per gram. Once both numbers are on that same basis, the second median is about 33 times the first. Equal weights can therefore differ substantially before you consider variation in the weight of individual nuts. The researchers examined how selenium in the soil and soil acidity were related to accumulation in the nuts.

Why a database value cannot identify a package

A separate study also observed variation in two commercially available batches. A food table can provide a reference amount for comparisons, but an amount from those studies does not establish the content of an individual nut. The available figures are not a basis for counting specific nuts as a known supplement amount. For comparisons at named everyday portions, see reference selenium figures for fish, eggs and oats.
