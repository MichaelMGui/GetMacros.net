McDonald’s lists 440 kcal for a standard Double Cheeseburger in the U.S. and 459 kcal for the standard item in Japan. That is a difference of 19 kcal for the named burgers. In Japan, the separately listed nighttime Double Cheeseburger variant has 657 kcal, so picking the correct row within the market also matters.

Match the country and variant first

On the Japanese nutrition list, the two names have different leading characters. The entry beginning with 倍 is a different menu item in the nighttime menu. Its number is 198 kcal above the standard Japan listing. If you log the U.S. figure for the Japan nighttime listing, you would understate the published number by 217 kcal.
