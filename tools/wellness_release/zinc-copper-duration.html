The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements explains that taking high amounts of zinc over weeks can interfere with copper absorption. The example of 50 mg or more over weeks in its summary is about repeated exposure. One label line, on its own, does not describe what someone took or for how long.

Keep the reference limit separate from a study example

The U.S. adult Tolerable Upper Intake Level for zinc cited by NIH is 40 mg per day. That value is a separate reference from the reported exposure range in the discussion of copper. The comparison does not pinpoint a number where every person will develop a problem.

The amount from all sources matters more than the impression created by a single package. See how to read elemental zinc on the label. Personal decisions about supplements or measured copper status belong with a qualified clinician.
