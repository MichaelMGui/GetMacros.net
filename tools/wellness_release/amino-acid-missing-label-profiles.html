You can compare listed grams of protein for the amounts you eat. Comparing digestible amino-acid quality is a separate calculation. If a package gives only total protein, that does not tell you how much lysine it contains or how digestible each amino acid is.

Do not borrow a score from a vaguely similar ingredient

A food and a protein isolate derived from it can differ in formulation and processing. Even a measured profile from a related entry needs to match the food. Keep missing fields marked unknown, compare grams on a shared serving basis, and say which extra data would make a stronger comparison possible.
