The NIH food table gives 1.1 mg vitamin B6 for a cup of canned chickpeas, 0.5 mg for three ounces of roasted chicken breast, and 0.4 mg for a medium banana. If you halve the chickpeas, they provide roughly the same amount as the full chicken portion shown here.

Calculate a subtotal from prepared portions

Half a cup of the canned chickpeas, 1½ ounces of the cooked chicken and half the medium banana give an estimated subtotal of 1.0 mg. The result uses the source’s rounded concentrations, so an extra decimal is not extra precision. Replace each ingredient with the actual preparation you use when a better record is available.
