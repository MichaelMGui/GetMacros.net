Hydration scenario game

Choose the response that fits the situation. The goal is not “drink the most”—it is to combine fluid, food, cooling, context, and emergency awareness.

Question 1 of 8Score: 0

Safety boundary

Confusion, seizure, collapse, loss of consciousness, severe weakness, or suspected heat stroke needs urgent emergency help. Do not force a confused or unconscious person to drink. This educational game cannot distinguish dehydration, overhydration, or heat illness in an individual.

Read the complete hydration and electrolyte collection.

How it works

A short set of prompts on this topic. Answer each prompt, read why the answer is what it is, and move on. There is no timer, no sign-up and nothing is saved — you can restart whenever you like.

What this covers

Each round gives you a claim to judge, then explains the reasoning behind the answer rather than just marking it right or wrong. Statements you will weigh up include:

  • Water to thirst; no special electrolyte product is automatically needed
  • Bring the biggest bottle and keep the original pace
  • They need to force more water
  • Exactly 0.8 L must be consumed every exercise hour forever
  • Every cup causes net dehydration
  • This proves severe dehydration
  • Its branding","Convenient fluid plus carbohydrate and sodium during prolonged losses
  • Make them finish a bottle

Why bother

Recognising a shaky nutrition claim is a more durable skill than memorising a food list, because the claims change every year and the reasoning does not. If you would rather read first, browse the guide library or try a quiz.
