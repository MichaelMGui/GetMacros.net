↗Cutting, maintaining or bulking?

Choose the portion for your energy needs. For a higher-calorie meal, add a useful side, grain, bread, dressing or larger entrée while keeping protein and overall meal quality in view. For a calorie deficit, use a smaller portion—not an automatically “better” food.

Diet-pattern options

  • Vegetarian: the listed boxes are vegetarian but contain egg and/or milk.
  • Vegan: packaged snack availability changes; verify the current ingredient label rather than assuming bakery food is vegan.
  • Higher protein: a protein box is a more complete option than pastry alone.
  • Lower added sugar: pair food with unsweetened coffee or tea and adjust flavoured syrup pumps.
  • Gluten-aware: packaged foods show allergens, but unpackaged items and shared equipment cannot be guaranteed allergen-free.

Caffeine belongs in the total

Size, roast, espresso shots and tea type change caffeine. If sleep, pregnancy, anxiety or migraine affects your limit, use the live beverage page.
