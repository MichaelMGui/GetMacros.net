Private label arithmetic

Carbs per portion calculator

Use the Nutrition Facts panel to calculate total carbohydrate in the portion you expect to eat. Entries stay in your browser.

This tool does not calculate insulin. Ratios, correction factors, timing and glucose targets must come from your diabetes team.

Use total carbohydrate first

Do not add sugars or fiber to total carbohydrate; they are components within it. Do not subtract fiber unless your diabetes education plan specifically tells you to use that method.
