PCOS, Weight Stigma, Body Image and Eating Disorders

PCOS can affect weight, skin, hair, fertility and identity. Care should improve health without humiliation, blame or an assumption that every concern is solved by weight loss.

Get immediate help: Fainting, chest pain, vomiting blood, severe dehydration, suicidal thoughts or an inability to stay safe requires urgent support. Eating disorders occur at every body size.

Weight stigma causes real harm

Being dismissed, weighed without consent or told to â€œjust lose weightâ€ can delay diagnosis and make care harder to return to. The international guideline specifically asks clinicians to recognize weight stigma and use shared decision-making.

Ask for respectful care

You can request the purpose of weighing, ask not to hear the number, explain that restrictive advice is harmful, or bring a support person. A useful visit addresses periods, glucose, lipids, blood pressure, sleep, mood, fertility goals and symptomsâ€”not just BMI.

Watch for disordered eating

Warning signs include skipping meals to compensate, feeling out of control around food, purging, compulsive exercise, intense fear of carbohydrates, secretive eating or self-worth tied to tracking. PCOS guidance recommends considering eating-disorder and disordered-eating risk, especially during lifestyle or weight management.

Regular nourishment can be treatment-supportive

A predictable meal pattern, permission to include enjoyable foods and less moral language may reduce the restrictâ€“binge cycle. A dietitian experienced in both PCOS and eating disorders can help adapt nutrition without reinforcing fear.

Body image deserves care too

Acne, hair loss and unwanted hair can be distressing; that distress is not vanity. Mental-health care, peer support and evidence-based medical or cosmetic options may all belong in a complete plan.

Choose goals beyond the scale

More stable energy, adequate fibre, strength, improved sleep, regular medication use and keeping screening appointments are meaningful outcomes. Health behaviour can matter even when weight is unchanged.
