Gut-health myth game

Safety boundary

This game cannot diagnose IBS, allergy, infection, celiac disease, inflammatory bowel disease, obstruction, or a probiotic need. Severe pain, significant bleeding, collapse, confusion, repeated vomiting with inability to hydrate, or signs of shock needs urgent care.

Read the complete collection.

How it works

A short set of prompts on this topic. Answer each prompt, read why the answer is what it is, and move on. There is no timer, no sign-up and nothing is saved — you can restart whenever you like.

What this covers

Each round gives you a claim to judge, then explains the reasoning behind the answer rather than just marking it right or wrong. Statements you will weigh up include:

  • Any gas proves food intolerance
  • It will treat every symptom
  • Avoid every FODMAP forever
  • Assume fibre is toxic","Add even more immediately
  • IBS and food intolerance","A universal diversity deficiency
  • Fast until every stool is perfect
  • Yes, fermentation guarantees a clinical strain and dose
  • A large fibre supplement","A detox tea

Why bother

Recognising a shaky nutrition claim is a more durable skill than memorising a food list, because the claims change every year and the reasoning does not. If you would rather read first, browse the guide library or try a quiz.
