Price is not nutritional quality

This calculator answers one narrow question: cost per 25 grams of listed protein. It does not rank foods overall. Beans may contribute fiber; dairy may contribute calcium; fish may contribute omega-3 fats; and preparation time or waste may decide what is actually affordable.
