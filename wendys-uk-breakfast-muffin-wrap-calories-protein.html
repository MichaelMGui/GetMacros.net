Wendy’s UK breakfast: muffins versus the egg-and-cheese wrap

Published October 18, 2026 · United Kingdom source servings

Wendy’s UK lists its Classic Egg & Cheese Muffin at 319 kcal and 14 g protein. The Egg & Cheese Breakfast Wrap with brown sauce is 290 kcal and 13 g protein. These are individual breakfast foods, with no potato side or drink added.

A wrap is not automatically a bigger breakfast

In this comparison, the egg-and-cheese wrap has 29 fewer kcal and 1 g less protein than the egg-and-cheese muffin. The wrap’s named brown-sauce recipe is 137 g; the muffin is 130 g. The difference is about their actual recipes, not a rule that wraps contain less energy than bread.

What do bacon and sausage change?

The Classic Bacon & Cheese Muffin lists 384 kcal and 19 g protein. The Classic Sausage & Cheese Muffin lists 477 kcal and 23 g protein. Moving from the egg-and-cheese muffin to the sausage-and-cheese one is a 158 kcal difference alongside 9 g more protein.

Those are separate menu recipes. This is not a calculation of what happens when a sausage is added to the egg muffin; the table does not establish which ingredients or amounts are held constant. Choose the exact name when logging rather than combining two complete sandwiches as if one were an add-on.

The egg-and-cheese wrap has 1.2 g salt in the named brown-sauce version; the egg-and-cheese muffin has 1 g. The lower-calorie choice is therefore not the lower-salt one. Similarly, the bacon-and-cheese muffin lists 1.7 g salt and the sausage-and-cheese muffin 1.4 g.

Use the published grams of salt when comparing this UK chart. A tool showing sodium will use different units; salt and sodium numbers cannot be placed in the same column without conversion.

Order, then log the whole breakfast

  1. Check that your Wendy’s branch serves breakfast. The official UK page says breakfast is not available at every location.
  2. Match the exact filling and sauce. A red-sauce wrap has its own source entry, even where the energy figure happens to match.
  3. Add any potato side, coffee, milk, sugar or other extra as its own supported portion. A sandwich value is not the total for a priced breakfast meal.

The nutrition chart is the UK edition dated 25 March 2026, read on 18 October 2026. Later product changes and branch availability are separate checks; a newer retrieval date does not turn the chart into a new recipe edition.
