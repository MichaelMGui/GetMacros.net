How to order at Sweetgreen

  • A salad name does not determine its calorie total; grains, nuts, cheese, avocado and dressing matter more.
  • For a meal rather than a side salad, check that the bowl contains a meaningful protein source and enough food for your appetite.
  • Plant-based bowls can be high in fiber but lower in protein. Add tofu or another listed protein if that is your priority.

Match the order to your goal

  • Cutting: start with the lighter substantial list, then choose a portion that keeps you satisfied.
  • Bulking: use the higher-energy list and favour options with meaningful protein.
  • High protein: compare grams of protein and protein per 100 calories; neither replaces total meal context.
  • Higher fiber: favor beans, vegetables, whole grains or other items where fiber is actually published.
