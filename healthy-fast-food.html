What makes a useful fast-food comparison?

Start with the exact order and portion. A grilled sandwich, a rice bowl and a breakfast wrap can each fit different preferences; the full order changes when you add fries, dressing, sauce or a drink. Compare calories and protein together, then check fiber and sodium where reported.

The finder currently supports 917 U.S. menu portions across 26 chains, including mains, sides, drinks and breakfast items. The lists below are selected examples, not a ranking of the healthiest restaurants or every item on today’s menu.

How to use these lists

These lists compare options in our database, not every item on each restaurant’s menu. The calorie lists start at 250 calories and exclude sides. High-protein picks have at least 25 g protein. These are selection rules, not an overall health score.

We use standard U.S. menu items. Missing nutrition values stay blank. Dietary labels do not guarantee allergy safety; check with the restaurant.
