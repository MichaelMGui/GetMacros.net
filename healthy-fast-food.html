Our method

“Healthy” is not one macro.

1

Start with the whole order

Entrée, sides, sauces and drinks all belong in the comparison.

2

Match the person

A small low-calorie item is not useful if it leaves someone under-fueled.

3

Name uncertainty

Missing public values stay missing. We do not invent restaurant macros.

4

Keep allergies separate

“Gluten-aware” or plant-based never guarantees freedom from cross-contact.
