How to read this guide

Useful numbers, honest limits.

“Healthy” depends on the person and the day. We organize choices by stated goals instead of pretending one meal is universally best.

See sources & methods
Restaurant data firstNutrition figures come from published restaurant information and are dated in each chain guide.
Missing means missingAn unpublished sodium or fibre value is labeled—not guessed, ranked as zero, or hidden.
Trade-offs stay visibleA high-protein meal can also be high in sodium. Both facts belong on the same card.
Availability changesMenus, portions and regional preparation vary. Confirm allergies and current nutrition with the restaurant.
