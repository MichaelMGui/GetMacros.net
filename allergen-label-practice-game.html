Emergency boundary

This game cannot diagnose an allergy or decide whether a product is safe for an individual. Follow the prescribed emergency action plan. Active breathing difficulty, throat symptoms, collapse, or suspected anaphylaxis needs prescribed epinephrine and emergency help as directed by that plan.

How it works

A short set of prompts on this topic. Answer each prompt, read why the answer is what it is, and move on. There is no timer, no sign-up and nothing is saved — you can restart whenever you like.

What this covers

Each round gives you a claim to judge, then explains the reasoning behind the answer rather than just marking it right or wrong. Statements you will weigh up include:

  • Milk, egg, fish, Crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, sesame
  • It is not declared","In parentheses after whey
  • The product contains a standardized safe amount
  • It still contains milk proteins
  • Wheat-free always means gluten-free
  • Heat reliably removes allergen protein
  • Recipes and suppliers can change
  • Keep the plan only in the nurse’s office

Why bother

Recognising a shaky nutrition claim is a more durable skill than memorising a food list, because the claims change every year and the reasoning does not. If you would rather read first, browse the guide library or try a quiz.
