↗Cutting, maintaining or bulking?

Choose the portion for your energy needs. For a higher-calorie meal, add a useful side, grain, bread, dressing or larger entrée while keeping protein and overall meal quality in view. For a calorie deficit, use a smaller portion—not an automatically “better” food.

Diet-pattern options

  • Vegetarian: choose the AVA-certified Veggie Bowl or swap meat for beans where available; certified vegetarian may still include dairy and egg.
  • Vegan: remove dairy-based sauces, cheese and sour cream, then verify rice, beans, tortilla and cross-contact in the current ingredient tool.
  • Higher protein: chicken plus beans creates two protein sources.
  • Lower sodium: request fewer sauces and cheese and compare the live customized total.
  • Gluten-aware: corn-based items may avoid a wheat ingredient, but shared preparation prevents a celiac-safe assumption.

Packets are separate decisions

The Cantina bowl page lists salsa packets separately. Add the nutrition for packets actually used; “included with” does not always mean included in the displayed total.
