Kirkland Canada: macadamias vs mixed nuts by portion

Saved guides →

By GetMacros · Published October 14, 2026

For the retained Canadian Kirkland retailer labels, a calculated 28 g macadamia portion has 218.4 kcal and 2.2 g protein; the listed 28 g Unsalted Mixed Nuts serving has 170 kcal and 5 g protein. Compare equal amounts before comparing the numbers printed beside different label servings.

The macadamia label serving is 50 g with 390 kcal and 4 g protein. The mixed-nut label uses 28 g with 170 kcal and 5 g protein. Comparing 390 directly with 170 would compare unequal portions. Macadamias at 28 g calculate as 390 × 28 ÷ 50 = 218.4 kcal; mixed nuts at 50 g calculate as 170 × 50 ÷ 28 = about 303.6 kcal.

Check the exact product

The records are Macadamia Nuts in a 680 g package and Unsalted Mixed Nuts in a 1.13 kg package. Different flavours, countries and formulations need their own labels. These retailer pages are not real-time warehouse inventory or proof that the package you bought is unchanged.

Do not infer a whole-container serving

The macadamia retailer page has a conflicting container-count field beside a 680 g package and 50 g serving. GetMacros does not use that count to establish portions. Weigh your amount or confirm the package label; a missing nutrient stays unknown rather than being filled from another nut product.

For your actual portion, open the exact food and change grams. Equal weight shows nutrient density; listed serving answers a different question. Neither is an automatic health ranking.
