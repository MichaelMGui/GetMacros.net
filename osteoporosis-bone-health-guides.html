Why bone health is more than calcium

Bone density, bone quality, falls, muscle, vision, medicines and health conditions shape fracture risk. A normal serum calcium value does not measure lifetime bone strength.

Editorial standard

These pages use USPSTF, NIH, FDA and NIAMS guidance, distinguish population screening from individual risk, and never present outside clinicians as authors or reviewers.
