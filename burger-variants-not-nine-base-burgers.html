In-N-Out burger variations: compare the exact build

Your shelf

Standard, sauce and Protein Style entries describe different servings.

In this guide
The variations deserve their own valuesCount the unit you actually meanWhat this means for browsing

By GetMacros · Published October 4, 2026

Our U.S. In-N-Out selection includes three documented versions of each of three base burgers. Each base has its standard-with-onion row, a published mustard-and-ketchup variation and a published Protein Style variation. Nine documented choices do not mean nine unrelated base burgers.

The variations deserve their own values

They are separate records because calories, macros, sodium or serving weight change. Choose the recipe you actually want: the standard, mustard-and-ketchup and lettuce-wrapped versions should not share one assumed nutrition total.

Count the unit you actually mean

Compare one named serving with another named serving. A lettuce wrap changes the build; it does not mean every custom topping or extra patty has a verified total. Nine entries here describe three base burgers with documented variations.

What this means for browsing

Seeing several versions can help you choose an explicitly supported build. It does not imply that every off-menu alteration has been calculated or that all options are available everywhere. Fries, drinks and additional packets are outside these nine burger rows.

The table shows the three-by-three structure in the January 2026 source. This is a classification of the inspected records, not a menu sales analysis or a claim about the popularity of lettuce wraps. Use the restaurant’s nutrition information for alterations outside these documented choices.
