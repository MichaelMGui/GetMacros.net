Plant milks compared: protein, calcium, sugar, and culinary use

Plant beverages are not nutritionally interchangeable with one another or automatically equivalent to cow’s milk.

By the GetMacros.net editorial team · Reviewed August 13, 2026 · General education, not individualized medical advice

Quick answer: Compare soy, oat, almond, pea, coconut, and other plant beverages by fortification, protein, added sugar, flavor, and intended use.

Protein varies widely

Fortified soy and some pea-based beverages often provide more protein than almond, oat, rice, or coconut beverages, but brands differ. Check the label rather than assuming from the plant name.

Fortification matters

Look for calcium and vitamin D, and consider vitamin B12 in vegan patterns. Shake products when directed because minerals can settle. Organic versions are not always fortified.

Choose for the job

Flavor and texture matter in coffee, cereal, sauces, and baking. For young children, beverage choices have special nutritional implications and should follow pediatric or public-health guidance rather than adult trends.
