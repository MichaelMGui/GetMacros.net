Compare three Panda Express chicken entrees

Compare source-defined Panda Express chicken portions and see what changes when the same full rice base is added to each.

By GetMacros · Published October 3, 2026

In the inspected U.S. table, Grilled Teriyaki Chicken has 38 g protein and 280 calories, String Bean Chicken has 15 g and 210 calories, and Orange Chicken has 19 g and 560 calories. These are the restaurant’s three different full entree portions.

Hold the rice portion constant

Panda’s full 12 oz white-rice base contributes 570 calories, 11 g protein and 129 g carbohydrate. Adding that same source portion to each entree gives these calculated orders:

  • Teriyaki chicken plus white rice: 850 calories and 49 g protein.
  • String bean chicken plus white rice: 780 calories and 26 g protein.
  • Orange chicken plus white rice: 1,130 calories and 30 g protein.

These are GetMacros sums, not three separately published combo totals. They assume full source-table portions with no other entree, sauce packet or drink. A half-side order is a different calculation.

A lower-calorie entree is not always the highest-protein one

String Bean Chicken has the fewest calories of these three portions, but Teriyaki Chicken has more than twice its protein. Orange Chicken has a different carbohydrate and fat profile as well. The table lets you choose the constraint rather than relying on the word “chicken.”

For the base decision, compare rice, chow mein and Super Greens portions. Panda’s separate teriyaki sauce entry is 70 calories per 1.8 oz; it is not silently included in these extra-sauce-free calculations.
