More tenders or a side with your chicken?

Five tenders provide more protein; three tenders with red beans and rice provide more calories and fiber. Their recorded sodium totals are close. This is a choice between a larger chicken portion and a mixed order, not a comparison where one option wins every category.

How to order at Popeyes

  • Tender count is the clearest portion control; sauce and biscuit are separate parts of the order.
  • Higher protein does not erase sodium or total energy, so use all three numbers for the decision.
  • Plant-based main options are limited in the tracked menu; do not infer suitability from a side without checking ingredients.
