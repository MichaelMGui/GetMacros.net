How it works

Clues come from the stored verified values. Unknown values are never treated as zero; source and serving are shown after solving.

Use touch or Tab and Enter. Restart repeats the puzzle; New puzzle changes it. There is no timer or mandatory sound.

Behind the game

Uses tracked U.S. menu data. The complete portion and official source appear after solving. Additional toppings and other markets are not implied.
