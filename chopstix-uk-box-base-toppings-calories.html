Chopstix UK calories: build the base and toppings together

Add the named base and each matching topping allocation. Regular Vegetable Chow Mein with the selected R/L/XL Caramel Drizzle and Sweet & Sour Chicken allocations totals 639 kcal and 29.5 g protein.

The base is not the finished box

Chopstix explicitly instructs customers to add selected toppings to the base’s nutrition. Its Regular Vegetable Chow Mein is 308 kcal; that does not include the chicken toppings in this worked example.

Build one explicit two-topping example

Regular noodles plus one R/L/XL Caramel Drizzle allocation and one R/L/XL Sweet & Sour allocation totals 308 + 169 + 162 = 639 kcal. Protein totals 9.3 + 10.5 + 9.7 = 29.5 g. The sauces belonging to the named chicken recipes are already inside those topping rows.

This is our component calculation, not a universal calorie label for every Regular box. An extra dip, another topping, a changed allocation or a different base needs its own row.

The base change is measurable

Using Regular Egg Fried Rice instead gives 796 kcal and 28.2 g protein. Regular Cauli Rice gives 389 kcal and 23.7 g protein. Those are differences for named portions, not equal weights of rice, noodles and vegetables.

The noodle-based example totals 5.08 g salt; egg fried rice 3.71 g; cauli rice 4.51 g. A change can affect calories, protein and salt differently. No overall health ranking follows from one column.

Small and Topper are not the R/L/XL amount

Caramel Drizzle Chicken has separate Small, R/L/XL and Topper columns. Small lists 253 kcal and Topper 327 kcal, compared with 169 kcal for R/L/XL. Choosing the wrong column can substantially misstate the box.

What if you cannot identify the allocation?

Keep the portion uncertain rather than substituting an arbitrary gram weight. The public columns name allocations without supplying reliable gram weights for these rows. Match the source’s size and restaurant serving rule, check with staff if needed, and log extra dips separately.
