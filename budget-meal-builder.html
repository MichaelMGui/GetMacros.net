Select at least one item, then build. Suggestions use broad ingredient roles rather than exact recipes.

Eight pantry combinations to keep

Tomato bean grain bowl

Beans + rice or grain + canned tomatoes + herbs, salsa, or cheese.

Peanut oat bowl

Oats + peanut or seed butter + shelf-stable milk or water + fruit.

Pantry pasta

Pasta + white beans or fish + tomatoes or vegetables + oil and herbs.

Quick lentil soup

Lentils + canned vegetables or tomatoes + broth or seasoning + bread.

Loaded potato

Potato + beans, fish, or egg + vegetables + salsa or cheese.

Cracker plate

Fish, hummus, or nut butter + crackers + fruit or vegetables.

Fried rice formula

Cooked rice + egg or tofu + frozen vegetables + suitable sauce.

Pantry wraps

Tortilla + beans or canned protein + vegetables + salsa or sauce.

These are frameworks, not prescriptions. Adapt for allergies, culture, medical needs, equipment, food safety, and what the household will eat.
