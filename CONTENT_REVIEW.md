# Content and asset review — 10 September 2026

## Findings

The restaurant pages and meal finder use nutrition figures attributed to restaurant sources, with meal selection, comparisons, calculations and explanatory notes added by GetMacros. That is different from simply republishing an article, but attribution and added value do not themselves grant rights to third-party material.

The article-generation files contain the site's explanatory copy. A search of the production tools did not identify a wholesale article scraper. Sample searches for three distinctive sentences did not reveal a clear matching third-party article. These were limited checks, not an exhaustive plagiarism comparison or proof of original authorship.

All 73 HTML files were scanned for external image and iframe embeds and the retired image references. No external image/iframe embeds or remaining retired references were found after this update. This does not mean the site makes no external requests: advertising and ordinary source links are separate.

No reuse licence record was found for the old restaurant logo PNGs or the recipe illustration WEBP. Published references now use new neutral restaurant initials and the site's meal-plate SVG, respectively. The WEBP reference was in structured data. The old files remain unreferenced for compatibility with earlier generation steps; the final build pass prevents their reintroduction into pages.

Font licence files are present for Manrope, Inter and Fraunces. Older local illustrations and raster assets still have incomplete independent provenance documentation. See `images/README.md` for the inventory and limitations.

## What Google requires

[Google's replicated-content policy](https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11190248?hl=en) prohibits ads on copied or embedded content that lacks added commentary, curation or other value. It also identifies automatically generated content without manual review or curation as an unacceptable example.

[Google's AdSense policy guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/2660562) separately prohibits copyright-infringing content. Adding commentary does not waive that requirement.

## Limits

This repository review cannot certify that every asset or sentence is infringement-free, resolve jurisdiction-specific rights, or predict AdSense approval. No claim that all text was written without AI assistance is made. Nutrition accuracy, publisher policies, advertising implementation and the quality of the overall experience are separate review considerations. Google makes the approval decision.
