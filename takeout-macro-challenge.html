Takeout Macro Challenge

Eight quick comparisons build the skill of reading the whole order.

How it works

Eight quick comparisons build the skill of reading the whole order. Answer each prompt, read why the answer is what it is, and move on. There is no timer, no sign-up and nothing is saved — you can restart whenever you like.

What this covers

Each round gives you a claim to judge, then explains the reasoning behind the answer rather than just marking it right or wrong. Statements you will weigh up include:

  • A salad is always lower in calories and sodium than a sandwich.
  • Gluten-aware means guaranteed safe for celiac disease.
  • Sauces and drinks belong in the nutrition total.
  • The lowest-calorie item is always the healthiest.
  • Official nutrition can change after customization.
  • Vegan automatically means high protein and allergen-free.
  • Sodium can be high even in grilled food.
  • Current restaurant data should be rechecked before medical use.

Why bother

Recognising a shaky nutrition claim is a more durable skill than memorising a food list, because the claims change every year and the reasoning does not. If you would rather read first, browse the guide library or try a quiz.
