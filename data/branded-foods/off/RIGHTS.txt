Open Food Facts source-separated derivative partition

The independently licensed USDA CC0, Canadian Nutrient File OGL, and factual retailer partitions are not incorporated into this derivative. No nutrient values are fused across providers.
Free machine-readable source subset: source-records.ndjson.gz. Free normalized export: records.ndjson.gz. Free normalized records and indexes: manifest.json and its named shards. Transformation algorithm: normalization.py. Retain these notices and share-alike requirements when redistributing this partition.

No product imagery is included. Brand names identify products; no partnership or endorsement is implied. Country tags are community-reported; no live retail inventory or independent package verification is claimed.
