GetMacros publishes a normalized, source-linked selection of the 2026 reference
file. This is a food-composition reference, not a current Canadian grocery label
database. Do not imply Government of Canada endorsement. The edition contains
mixed analytical, label, imputed and calculated nutrient values; the original
per-nutrient source descriptions and dates remain attached to each profile.

2026 measure-weight conversions already express gram weights. They must not be
multiplied by 100 as if they were the previous 2015 conversion factors.
