Endometriosis Nutrition Guides

Nutrition can support strength, bowel comfort and iron status. It cannot diagnose endometriosis, remove lesions or replace pain, hormonal, surgical or fertility care.

Urgent boundary: Sudden severe pain, fainting, fever, repeated vomiting, very heavy bleeding or pain and bleeding in a possible pregnancy needs urgent assessment.
