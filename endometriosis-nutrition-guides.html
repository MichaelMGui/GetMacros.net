Endometriosis nutrition

Endometriosis Nutrition Guides

Nutrition can support strength, bowel comfort and iron status. It cannot diagnose endometriosis, remove lesions or replace pain, hormonal, surgical or fertility care.

Urgent boundary: Sudden severe pain, fainting, fever, repeated vomiting, very heavy bleeding or pain and bleeding in a possible pregnancy needs urgent assessment.

Current diagnostic context

ACOG’s 2026 guidance supports a clinical diagnosis based on history, symptoms and examination so treatment can begin while evaluation continues. NICE recommends ultrasound for suspected endometriosis but states that a normal examination or scan does not exclude it.

Editorial standard

These pages use ACOG, NICE and ESHRE guidance, state where diet evidence is limited, and never present outside clinicians as authors or reviewers.
