What this collection does differently

It separates useful daily habits from medical diagnosis, fertility treatment and supplement marketing. It recognizes that PCOS occurs across body sizes and that healthy behaviours can help even without weight loss.

When to get care quickly

Seek urgent help for very heavy bleeding with dizziness, fainting, severe pelvic pain, pregnancy-related pain or bleeding, chest pain, breathing trouble, confusion or an inability to stay safe.
