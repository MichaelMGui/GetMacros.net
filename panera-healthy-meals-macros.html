Panera Balanced Meals and Macros

Panera offers vegetables, grains and protein, but full salads and bowls can carry substantial sodium. Half portions can be useful when paired deliberately—not automatically “healthier.”

↗Cutting, maintaining or bulking?

Choose the portion for your energy needs. For a higher-calorie meal, add a useful side, grain, bread, dressing or larger entrée while keeping protein and overall meal quality in view. For a calorie deficit, use a smaller portion—not an automatically “better” food.

Diet-pattern options

  • Vegetarian: Mediterranean Greens with Grains provides vegetables and fibre but less protein than the chicken version.
  • Higher protein: the chicken version reaches 35 g in the whole serving.
  • Lower sodium: a half portion, less dressing and skipping a salty side can reduce the total.
  • Vegan: cheese, dressings, bread and broth ingredients require current verification and customization.
  • Gluten-aware: salads and bowls still require ingredient and cross-contact checks.

The side belongs in the order

Baguette, chips or fruit change carbohydrate, sodium, fibre and energy. Select the side based on the complete meal and appetite.
