Why the cheese amount changes

The source has one cheese quantity for a cheese-only pizza and another for cheese alongside toppings. Using the cheese-only quantity for a pepperoni pizza would describe a different build.

For the listed cheese pizza: 120 kcal crust + 5 kcal pizza sauce + 70 kcal regular cheese = 195 kcal per slice. Six matching slices total 1,170 kcal. These are component sums, not a separately published complete-pizza test.
