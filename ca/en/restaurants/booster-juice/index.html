Total sugar is not added sugar

The guide reports total sugar. It does not split naturally occurring sugar from added sugar, so neither a fruit recipe nor a sorbet-free description establishes a zero-added-sugar or zero-total-sugar value.

Volume is also not weight: 710 mL is not treated as 710 g. Custom milk, fruit or booster changes need their own published portion information.
