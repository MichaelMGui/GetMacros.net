Why some large orders are absent

The guide gives several large sides and buckets a multi-serving label without an unambiguous total-order denominator. We do not multiply those rows into an invented full bucket. A large-Taters fat value also exceeds its listed mass, and two spicy-tender sodium figures need clarification. These rows are withheld.
