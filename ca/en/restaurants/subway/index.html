Logging a footlong

Subway defines a footlong as two 6-inch servings for the listed sub recipes. For the Fresh Fit Rotisserie-Style Chicken example, that gives 900 kcal and 60 g protein. A wrap, bowl or snackwich is not doubled under that rule.

Salad values include the named vegetables and exclude dressing unless noted. The breakfast groups include their source-listed bread or wrap, egg and cheese assumptions; confirm the exact menu name.
