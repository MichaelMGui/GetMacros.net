Migraine nutrition

Migraine Nutrition Guides

Use nutrition to support a steadier baseline without blaming every attack on food or replacing neurological care.

Emergency boundary: Sudden worst-ever headache, new weakness, confusion, seizure, fever with stiff neck, head injury, or new severe symptoms in pregnancy requires urgent assessment.

Migraine Diet and Balanced Meals

Build regular, adequate meals without a universal trigger list, fear-based restriction or migraine-cure claims.

Migraine Food Triggers, Diaries and Elimination Diets

Test suspected food patterns one at a time without turning a headache diary into permanent restriction.

Migraine, Caffeine, Coffee and Withdrawal

Understand why caffeine can relieve, trigger or perpetuate headaches and how to change intake gradually.

Migraine Hydration and Electrolytes

Use thirst, routine and context to support hydration without forcing water or turning electrolytes into a migraine cure.

Migraine Supplements: Magnesium, Riboflavin and CoQ10

Evaluate preventive supplement evidence, doses, side effects, interactions and product quality without calling natural risk-free.

Migraine Nausea, Vomiting and Flare-Day Food

Protect fluids and nourishment during attacks while recognizing when vomiting or neurological symptoms need urgent care.

Migraine pattern diary

Create a private visit-ready snapshot of timing, context and treatment response.

Trigger myth game

Practice eight food, hydration, caffeine, supplement and emergency decisions.

What this collection avoids

No universal trigger list, detox, food-sensitivity panel or supplement stack can diagnose or cure migraine. Patterns should be tested carefully while maintaining adequate nutrition.

Editorial standard

These pages use NINDS, NIH, American Migraine Foundation and American Headache Society guidance without presenting outside clinicians as authors or reviewers.
