How to order at Wendy’s

  • Baked potatoes work as a carbohydrate side or base; toppings determine whether they stay simple or become a larger entrée.
  • For salads, include the dressing and crunchy toppings you actually use.
  • Compare single and larger burgers by both protein and total energy rather than assuming the biggest item is required for a high-protein meal.

Match the order to your goal

  • Cutting: start with the lighter substantial list, then choose a portion that keeps you satisfied.
  • Bulking: use the higher-energy list and favour options with meaningful protein.
  • High protein: compare grams of protein and protein per 100 calories; neither replaces total meal context.
  • Higher fibre: favour beans, vegetables, whole grains or other items where fibre is actually published.
