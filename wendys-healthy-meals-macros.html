↗Cutting, maintaining or bulking?

Choose the portion for your energy needs. For a higher-calorie meal, add a useful side, grain, bread, dressing or larger entrée while keeping protein and overall meal quality in view. For a calorie deficit, use a smaller portion—not an automatically “better” food.

Diet-pattern options

  • Higher protein: either listed salad provides 30 g or more in the current menu snapshot.
  • Lower energy: dressing and toppings can be adjusted, but eating too little is not automatically healthier.
  • Vegetarian: a plain baked potato and selected sides can form part of a meal; verify cooking and topping ingredients.
  • Lower sodium: bacon, cheese, dressing and seasoned entrées add up; use the current calculator.
  • Gluten-aware: removing bread or crispy toppings does not settle cross-contact risk.

Why some cells say “verify live”

Wendy’s menu pages expose calories and selected protein claims more consistently than a stable downloadable full table. We do not infer missing macros.
