A wrap and sandwich with the same protein

Both recorded items contain the same amount of protein. The wrap has fewer calories and less sodium, while the sandwich has more fiber. That is a useful comparison when protein is your first filter: more calories here do not buy more protein.

How to order at Wendy’s

  • Start with the tracked sandwich, salad or chili portion, then account for any extras.
  • For salads, include the dressing and crunchy toppings you actually use.
  • Compare single and larger burgers by both protein and total energy rather than assuming the biggest item is required for a high-protein meal.
