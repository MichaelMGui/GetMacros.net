# Search intent and quality plan — 7 September 2026

Research used Google Search Central's SEO Starter Guide and Google AdSense's site-readiness guidance, plus current results for fast-food nutrition and macro calculators. This is qualitative intent research, not measured keyword volume or a traffic forecast. Search Console and keyword-planner data have not been provided.

## Implementation principles

Use descriptive unique titles and descriptions, correct canonical URLs, working internal links, readable headings, useful source citations and truthful authorship. Preserve established URLs and avoid publishing duplicate topic pages. Google does not use the meta-keywords tag; repeating keywords unnaturally is counterproductive. No extra meta-keywords tags are planned.

The current 72 indexable URLs are represented in the sitemap and site search. Outdated structured breadcrumb links to removed hubs were repaired. Tool metadata now points to the calculator hub; visible breadcrumbs must remain consistent with it.

AdSense readiness depends on original useful content, clear navigation, and compliance with Google's policies. No change here establishes approval, and ad placement still needs review with the live account after a production release. Local browser tests block third-party ads for reproducibility.
