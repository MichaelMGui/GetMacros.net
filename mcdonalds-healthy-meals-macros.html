↗Cutting, maintaining or bulking?

Choose the portion for your energy needs. For a higher-calorie meal, add a useful side, grain, bread, dressing or larger entrée while keeping protein and overall meal quality in view. For a calorie deficit, use a smaller portion—not an automatically “better” food.

Diet-pattern options

  • Portion-aware: a hamburger plus apple slices and water is a smaller meal; add food if it does not meet your energy needs.
  • Higher protein breakfast: Egg McMuffin provides 17 g before customization.
  • Vegetarian or vegan: U.S. meal options are limited and preparation ingredients can change. Use the live ingredient list rather than assuming fries or breakfast items qualify.
  • Lower sodium: most entrées contribute meaningful sodium; compare complete orders and customizations.
  • Gluten-aware: removing a bun changes ingredients but does not guarantee freedom from gluten cross-contact.

Do not confuse “small” with nutritionally complete

Lower calories can simply mean less food. For a full meal, consider protein, carbohydrate, produce, satisfaction and the time until the next eating opportunity.
