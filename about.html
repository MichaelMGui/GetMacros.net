How it works

From a published menu to a useful comparison

The method is deliberately simple enough to inspect.

01

Start with the official source

Restaurant figures come from each chain’s published nutrition information. Every guide links back to it and shows when it was checked.

02

Keep the whole order visible

Combined meals name every included item. Missing sodium, fiber or another value stays missing—it never becomes zero.

03

Rank for the user’s goal

Cutting, bulking and high-protein preferences change the order. Calories, protein, fiber and sodium remain visible together.

The standard

Useful without pretending to be perfect

  • Estimates are labelled as estimates. Calculator outputs are starting points, not prescriptions.
  • Trade-offs stay visible. High protein does not erase calories or sodium.
  • Bylines stay honest. GetMacros does not invent doctors, dietitians or fake expert reviewers.
  • Corrections have a route. A specific error can be reported and logged.

Important boundary

Education, not personal medical care

GetMacros is built for generally healthy adults making everyday food decisions. It does not diagnose conditions or replace a clinician.

General calculators may be the wrong tool during pregnancy, growth, eating-disorder recovery or when a health condition changes nutrition needs.
