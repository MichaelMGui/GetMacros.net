LEON UK chicken lunch: wrap or Big Box?

The Aioli Chicken Big Box lists 29 g protein versus 27 g in the Chicken Aioli Wrap, with 770 versus 566 kcal and 481 versus 266 g total food. Format and full recipe matter.

The Aioli wrap and Big Box are different lunches

LEON’s Chicken Aioli Wrap is 266 g, 566 kcal and 27 g protein. The Aioli Chicken Big Box is 481 g, 770 kcal and 29 g protein. The box adds 204 kcal and 2 g protein, while supplying a much larger total food portion.

Look beyond the word “chicken”

The wrap combines chicken with khobez bread, aioli and salad ingredients. The Big Box is a rice-based recipe with slaw, seeds and aioli. It is not the same wrap with bread removed, nor does the 2 g protein difference measure the amount of extra chicken.

The complete source weights differ by 215 g. Much of a larger meal can come from ingredients other than its named protein. Use the full nutrition table rather than assuming a larger box has proportionally more protein.

A spicy wrap offers another distinct recipe

The Spicy Chicken Zhoug Wrap lists 479 kcal, 29 g protein and 4 g fibre. It has the same listed protein as the Aioli Chicken Big Box, with 291 fewer kcal in its 263 g portion. That does not make it the same amount of food or guarantee the same fullness.

The Satay Chicken Big Box lists 776 kcal and 32 g protein for 507 g. Its source ingredients include peanut satay sauce and fish sauce. A different sauce can change ingredients and allergy considerations even when the calories are close to another box.

Fibre and salt answer different questions

The Aioli wrap has 2.7 g fibre and 1.45 g salt; the Aioli Big Box has 5.8 g fibre and 2.65 g salt. The larger recipe adds fibre and also more salt. Those trade-offs are more informative than calling every rice box a better choice than every wrap.

Choose the exact entry

LEON also lists a Chargrilled Chicken Rice Box aimed at children. It is not the adult Aioli Big Box and is deliberately excluded here. Check the recipe and portion name, then add any extra side, drink or sauce separately. The figures are whole named recipes, not a customization calculator.
