Activity examples

Mostly sitting: little exercise. Lightly active: exercise 1–3 days a week. Moderately active: 3–5 days. Very active: 6–7 days. Choose physical job + training if both are part of your routine.

How goals change the estimate

Lose fat + build muscle uses a smaller calorie deficit and higher protein. Muscle gain also depends on training, recovery and experience. Fat is set to 30% of calories; the fat calculator shows a wider reference range.
