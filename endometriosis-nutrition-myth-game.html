How it works

Eight decisions test cure claims, bowel diets, iron safety, diagnosis and urgent symptoms. Your score stays in this page. Answer each prompt, read why the answer is what it is, and move on. There is no timer, no sign-up and nothing is saved — you can restart whenever you like.

What this covers

Each round gives you a claim to judge, then explains the reasoning behind the answer rather than just marking it right or wrong. Statements you will weigh up include:

  • A special diet can remove endometriosis lesions.
  • A normal ultrasound always excludes endometriosis.
  • Low FODMAP is intended as permanent maximum restriction.
  • Heavy bleeding can cause iron deficiency.
  • Gluten must be removed for every person with endometriosis.
  • Food can support health even when it does not treat lesions.
  • Supplements marketed for estrogen detox are proven cures.
  • Sudden severe pain with fainting or possible pregnancy needs urgent care.

Why bother

Recognising a shaky nutrition claim is a more durable skill than memorising a food list, because the claims change every year and the reasoning does not. If you would rather read first, browse the guide library or try a quiz.
