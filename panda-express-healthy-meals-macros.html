↗Cutting, maintaining or bulking?

Choose the portion for your energy needs. For a higher-calorie meal, add a useful side, grain, bread, dressing or larger entrée while keeping protein and overall meal quality in view. For a calorie deficit, use a smaller portion—not an automatically “better” food.

Diet-pattern options

  • Higher protein: Grilled Teriyaki Chicken supplies 33 g before adding the side.
  • More vegetables and fibre: Super Greens contributes 5 g fibre in the listed portion.
  • Lower energy: an entrée with Super Greens can be lighter than a two-entrée plate, but appetite still matters.
  • Vegetarian or vegan: menu items and regional availability change; sauces may contain animal-derived ingredients. Verify the live allergen and ingredient data.
  • Gluten-aware: soy sauce, wheat and shared wok equipment are central cross-contact concerns.

Add components deliberately

A plate combines a side with one or more entrées. Add the published values for the exact components and portions rather than using one entrée’s number for the whole plate.
