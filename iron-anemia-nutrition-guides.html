Iron and anemia nutrition guides

Understand iron food sources and treatment questions while remembering that anemia has multiple causes and unexplained deficiency can signal bleeding or disease.

Emergency boundary: Chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, racing heart at rest, vomiting blood, black stool or uncontrolled bleeding needs urgent care.

Editorial standard

These pages cite NIH, NHLBI and NIDDK guidance. They never diagnose anemia from symptoms, recommend unsupervised high-dose iron or represent an outside physician as an author.
