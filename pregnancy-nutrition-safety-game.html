Pregnancy nutrition safety game

Choose the strongest answer, then learn why. This is educational practice—not medical clearance for a food, supplement or symptom.

Read the complete pregnancy and breastfeeding collection

How it works

Choose the strongest answer, then learn why. This is educational practice—not medical clearance for a food, supplement or symptom. Answer each prompt, read why the answer is what it is, and move on. There is no timer, no sign-up and nothing is saved — you can restart whenever you like.

What this covers

Each round gives you a claim to judge, then explains the reasoning behind the answer rather than just marking it right or wrong. Statements you will weigh up include:

  • A bottle says “complete prenatal” on the front. What is the best next step?
  • Trust the front claim","Read serving size and Supplement Facts
  • Which statement about fish is most accurate?
  • All fish should be avoided
  • A cheese label does not say pasteurized. What is the safer move?
  • Assume it is pasteurized","Verify before eating
  • Severe vomiting means very little urine and dizziness. What now?
  • Wait several days","Seek prompt medical care

Why bother

Recognising a shaky nutrition claim is a more durable skill than memorising a food list, because the claims change every year and the reasoning does not. If you would rather read first, browse the guide library or try a quiz.
