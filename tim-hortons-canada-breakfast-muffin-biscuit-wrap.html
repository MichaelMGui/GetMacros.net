Tim Hortons breakfast calories and protein in Canada

The Egg & Cheese English Muffin lists 270 kcal and 14 g protein; the biscuit has 350 kcal and the same protein. A Bacon Farmer’s Wrap is a different, larger listed order at 520 kcal and 19 g protein.

Choose the bread before comparing the filling

The egg-and-cheese English muffin and homestyle biscuit both list 14 g protein. The biscuit lists 350 kcal against the muffin’s 270: an 80 kcal difference without more listed protein. Carbohydrate changes from 27 g to 33 g, fat from 12 g to 18 g, and sodium from 500 mg to 720 mg.

This comparison is between the complete named sandwiches. It does not isolate the bread’s nutrition, because the published servings are finished recipes, not a controlled bread-only swap.

What changes with bacon or sausage?

For the English muffins in this Canadian guide, bacon lists 330 kcal and 17 g protein; sausage lists 450 kcal and 19 g protein. Choosing sausage adds 120 kcal and 2 g protein relative to the bacon sandwich. It does not follow that the sausage version is higher in every nutrient: sodium is 680 mg versus 710 mg for bacon.

A Farmer’s Wrap is another recipe

The Bacon Farmer’s Wrap is 520 kcal and 19 g protein; the Sausage Farmer’s Wrap is 640 kcal and 21 g protein. Both exceed their named English-muffin counterparts by 190 kcal in this edition. Use the wrap’s complete row when logging it. Do not add a hypothetical hash brown inside the wrap again, or subtract an assumed amount for an omitted sauce.

Count the breakfast you actually ordered

An egg-and-cheese English muffin, a separate Hash Brown and a medium Original Blend Coffee without additions total 394 kcal and 15 g protein: 270 + 120 + 4 kcal. The table below is our calculation, not a restaurant-advertised combo. Cream, sugar, a different drink or another side changes it.
