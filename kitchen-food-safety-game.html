Kitchen food-safety game

Choose what to refrigerate, thaw, measure, refreeze, reheat, or discard. Every answer explains the time-and-temperature rule behind it.

Safety boundary

This game uses U.S. household guidance and cannot clear food with unknown history, a recall, flood exposure, damaged packaging, home-canning risk, or special clinical restrictions. Never taste food to determine safety.

Read the complete collection.

How it works

A short set of prompts on this topic. Answer each prompt, read why the answer is what it is, and move on. There is no timer, no sign-up and nothing is saved — you can restart whenever you like.

What this covers

Each round gives you a claim to judge, then explains the reasoning behind the answer rather than just marking it right or wrong. Statements you will weigh up include:

  • Boil it and refrigerate","Discard it
  • Above ready-to-eat salad","In the door
  • A three-minute rest","Immediate ice bath
  • Cook it immediately","Store it raw for three days
  • It is definitely dangerous
  • Taste a bite","Discard it
  • Refreeze or cook it","Discard every package automatically
  • Pathogens may not change smell or appearance

Why bother

Recognising a shaky nutrition claim is a more durable skill than memorising a food list, because the claims change every year and the reasoning does not. If you would rather read first, browse the guide library or try a quiz.
