PCOS, Insulin Resistance and Carbohydrates

Insulin resistance is important in PCOS, but it does not mean every person must eliminate carbohydrates or continuously monitor glucose.

Clinical boundary: Home symptoms and supplement marketing cannot diagnose insulin resistance. Ask a clinician which validated glucose test is appropriate; routine insulin assays have limited clinical value in PCOS.

What insulin resistance means

Insulin helps move glucose from blood into cells. When cells respond less effectively, the body may produce more insulin. PCOS is associated with higher risk of impaired glucose tolerance and type 2 diabetes across body sizes, so periodic screening belongs in healthcare—not in a food guilt system.

Keep useful carbohydrates

Oats, barley, beans, lentils, fruit, yogurt, corn, potatoes and whole-grain breads can supply fibre and micronutrients. A food does not become “bad” because it raises glucose. The amount, preparation, meal context, activity and individual response all matter.

Pair for satisfaction

Add protein or fat to a carbohydrate when practical: fruit with nuts, toast with eggs, rice with beans and vegetables, or crackers with hummus. Pairing can slow digestion and make meals last longer, but it is an option—not a rule that makes eating fruit alone unsafe.

Glycemic index has limits

GI ranks foods under standardized conditions, not mixed meals in real life. Ripeness, cooking and portion change the response. Use it as one clue if helpful; fibre, nutrition, enjoyment and affordability still count.

Screening is more valuable than guessing

The guideline recommends assessing glycemic status at diagnosis and repeating it based on individual risk. A 75-gram oral glucose tolerance test is considered the most accurate option in PCOS; fasting glucose or HbA1c may be used when it cannot be done, with reduced accuracy.

Know urgent symptoms

Marked thirst, frequent urination, vomiting, confusion or unexplained rapid weight loss need prompt medical advice. Do not try to correct a possible glucose emergency with a restrictive diet.
