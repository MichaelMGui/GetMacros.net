How to use this collection

Start with the guide that matches a real decision: a grocery comparison, a lab conversation, or one meal you repeat. Keep medicines unchanged, bring supplement and salt-substitute labels to care visits, and use laboratory tests and blood-pressure technique—not symptoms alone—to evaluate risk.
