Nuggets or a sandwich?

The eight-count nuggets are a smaller protein portion. The sandwich includes a bun and supplies more total food energy, with only a small increase in protein. Neither number includes an extra side or a drink. A nugget serving and a sandwich answer different appetite needs; the smaller number is not automatically a better lunch.

How to order at Chick-fil-A

  • Compare the nugget count, then include any sides or sauces you plan to order.
  • Salad dressing and dipping sauces are separate items. Add the packet you actually use, not an assumed amount.
  • Breakfast sandwiches and lunch entrées solve different appetite needs; compare them within the meal you are buying.
