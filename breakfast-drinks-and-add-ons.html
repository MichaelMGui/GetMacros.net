Compare the breakfast you will actually order

Your shelf

Build a complete breakfast comparison instead of judging a sandwich alone. A worked order example includes a drink and side with clear source checks.

In this guide

By GetMacros · Published October 3, 2026

A breakfast sandwich’s nutrition may describe only the sandwich. Your drink, spread, sauce or side can make the finished order different. Check each item before comparing two breakfasts.

FDA’s menu-labeling guide includes beverages and meals with different choices. For a customized coffee, use the restaurant’s value for that size and preparation rather than assuming every coffee is identical.

Two hypothetical breakfasts

The values below are invented for a worked comparison. They are not current prices or nutrition from any chain.

ItemBreakfast ABreakfast B
Main item330 calories, 22 g protein400 calories, 25 g protein
Chosen drink170 calories, 6 g protein10 calories, 0 g protein
Chosen side120 calories, 2 g protein80 calories, 1 g protein
Whole breakfast620 calories, 30 g protein490 calories, 26 g protein

A’s main item starts lower in calories, but its complete breakfast finishes 130 calories higher. It also has 4 g more protein. Those are two useful facts; they do not establish one universally better breakfast.

Read the drink line carefully

  • Match the cup size and hot or iced version.
  • Check whether the published value includes milk, sweetener and standard flavorings.
  • Use supported customization values for a different milk or syrup amount.
  • Count a drink once if it is already included in a listed breakfast combination.

Do the same for spreads and condiments. A plain item, an item with a standard spread, and one with extra spread are different orders. Do not subtract an ingredient’s guessed calories from an official total and call the result verified.

Check availability as well as numbers

Breakfast hours and menu items can differ by location. GetMacros tracks selected U.S. orders, not every breakfast sold everywhere. Use the guide’s official restaurant source and confirm the local menu.

Compare tracked Dunkin’ breakfasts or see tracked Starbucks food orders. In the finder, choose Breakfast and keep the complete order description visible when comparing.
