Prenatal supplement label checker

Check what your label mentions and create a focused question list. Nothing you select is sent or stored.

This tool does not score safety or recommend doses. Pregnancy needs depend on diet, labs, medicines and health history. Review the actual bottle with a prenatal clinician or pharmacist.
What does the Supplement Facts panel list?







How to use the result

Photograph the front label, Supplement Facts and ingredient list. Bring the product plus every medicine and supplement you use. Missing an item does not automatically make a product unsuitable, and seeing an item does not establish that its dose is right.
