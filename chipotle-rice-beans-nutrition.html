Compare Chipotle rice and bean portions

Compare Chipotle’s published 4 oz rice and bean portions, including fiber, protein and sodium. See two explicit rice-and-bean sums.

By GetMacros · Published October 3, 2026

In Chipotle’s printed U.S. ingredient table, both white and brown rice are 210 calories per 4 oz. Black and pinto beans are 130 calories per 4 oz. Equal rice calories do not mean every nutrient matches.

Two rice-and-bean combinations

One white-rice portion plus one black-bean portion totals 340 calories, 12 g protein, 62 g carbohydrate, 8 g fiber and 560 mg sodium. One brown-rice portion plus one black-bean portion totals 340 calories, 12 g protein, 58 g carbohydrate, 9 g fiber and 400 mg sodium.

These are ingredient sums. Neither includes chicken, sofritas, salsa, vegetables, cheese or guacamole. Adding one of those means it is no longer a rice-and-beans-only total.

Beans are not just another rice serving

Both bean portions have 8 g protein, compared with either rice’s 4 g. Pinto beans have 8 g fiber versus black beans’ 7 g in the printed figures. That one-gram difference comes from rounded published portions, not a guarantee about each scoop.

If you order light rice, write down the assumed portion. A GetMacros half-portion calculation is not a measured counter scoop. The ingredient PDF is printed October 2024, even though its URL contains 2025 and we inspected it during this release.
