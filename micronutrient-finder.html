What this finder does not do

It does not diagnose deficiency, rank supplements, or calculate an individual dose. Food-source lists are examples, not exhaustive rankings. Needs and risks change with age, pregnancy, medicines, kidney or liver function, absorption, and other clinical factors.

Each linked guide distinguishes established functions from marketing claims and links to a primary government reference.
