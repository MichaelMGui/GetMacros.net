A note on how these were compiled: these are well-established, long-standing pages from Harvard's Nutrition Source, the U.S. National Institutes of Health, the National Academies' Dietary Reference Intakes, PubMed/PMC, Mayo Clinic, and Cleveland Clinic — sources chosen specifically for their track record of accuracy and stability. If a link ever moves, search the organization's site directly for the article title listed.
