Nutrition sources and calculation methods

This page explains the core references behind our nutrition tools. Individual articles and restaurant guides include their own sources, which provide the context for specific claims and menu figures.

Restaurant data and rankings

Each restaurant guide links to the chain’s official nutrition information. Figures describe the named standard build, not every possible substitution. Location, portioning and menu changes can affect your order.

The meal finder ranks the menu options in our dataset against your chosen preferences. It does not search every item a restaurant sells. Unconfirmed values stay unknown; the finder excludes incomplete records by default and lets you include them explicitly.

A high-protein match is not automatically a low-calorie or low-sodium meal. Read the complete numbers and the trade-offs on each result, and check the restaurant’s allergen information before ordering.

Find the source behind a claim. References below are grouped by topic. Individual articles explain which findings they use and where the evidence is limited. If a link moves, search the publisher’s site for the listed title.

How sources become articles

A citation supports a claim; it does not imply endorsement or authorship by the source. Read the complete editorial and source methodology, including evidence hierarchy and medical boundaries. Material factual changes appear in the public corrections log.
